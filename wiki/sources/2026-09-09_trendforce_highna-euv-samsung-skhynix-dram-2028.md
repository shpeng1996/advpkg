# TrendForce: The Race to 2028 — Samsung, SK hynix Set High-NA EUV Timelines for DRAM

## 核心主張 / Key Claims

- Samsung and SK hynix both targeting **High-NA EUV in DRAM production by 2028**
- SK hynix 1c DRAM: 13% of bit mix Q2'26 → **34% by Q4'26**; 1c = HBM4E core die (mass production 2027)
- High-NA EUV (NA 0.55 vs 0.33 EUV) can halve multipatterning steps, significantly cutting DRAM production cost
- SK hynix considering joining TSMC's **Large Size Mask Consortium** (12-inch photomasks)
- Micron recruiting for High-NA EUV equipment role at Albany NY Creates site
