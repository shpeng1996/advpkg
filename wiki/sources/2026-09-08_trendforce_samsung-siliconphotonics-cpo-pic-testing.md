# TrendForce: Samsung Reportedly Eyes In-House Silicon Photonics PIC Testing by End-2026

## 核心主張 / Key Claims

- Samsung bringing SiPh PIC wafer testing in-house by **end-2026** using TSMC-qualified probe stations (FormFactor/MPI)
- Samsung SiPh foundry services launch **2027** (PDKs, wafer-level PIC manufacturing)
- Samsung mass production for optical communications module maker begins **2H26**
- Long-term goal: integrate optical engines with logic chips and HBM; expand to GPUs/CPUs
- CPO switch market volume ramp **2027–2028** (TrendForce)
