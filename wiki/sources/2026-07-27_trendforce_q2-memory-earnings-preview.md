# Q2 Memory Earnings Preview: Samsung / SK hynix / Kioxia

## 核心主張 / Key Claims

- **Samsung Q2 2026 preliminary**: Revenue KRW 171T; operating profit ~18x YoY record. Consensus KRW 86T, best case KRW 90T (51% op margin). Would be highest quarterly profit ever by a global tech company, surpassing Nvidia fiscal 1Q26.
- **Samsung semiconductor head Kim Yong-kwan**: 2026 profit alone will exceed cumulative 40-year semiconductor profit.
- **SK hynix Q2 2026 (reports July 29)**: Revenue KRW 84.1T; operating profit KRW 64.1T; margin 75–77% (vs 72% in Q1). If realized, SK hynix surpasses TSMC in operating profit margin. LTA-driven sales to AI/cloud customers rising to 70% of revenue.
- **Kioxia Q2 (reports July 31)**: Market expects profit to double QoQ.
- **Samsung HBM strategy**: Deepening Google partnership; expanding HBM4 and HBM4E to NVIDIA; targeting HBM leadership reclaim by 2027.
