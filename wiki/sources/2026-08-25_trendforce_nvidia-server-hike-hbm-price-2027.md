# NVIDIA 15% Server Hike — HBM Price +50–79% in 2027 / TrendForce

## 核心主張 / Key Claims

- NVIDIA will raise Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell server prices **>15% in early 2027** (Bloomberg), passing higher memory costs to hyperscaler customers (Microsoft, Google, Oracle)
- Analysts expect HBM contract prices to rise **>50%** in 2027 vs 2026 (Bloter); UBS projects **+79% YoY HBM ASP** in 2027 — HBM4 and HBM4e pricing stronger than expected
- HBM profitability currently **trails DDR5 by ~40 percentage points** — DDR5 prices nearly quadrupled in past year; HBM locked into annual contracts, slow to reflect spot market
- NVIDIA still maintains **~75–80% gross margin** even if HBM4 costs double from HBM3e — ceiling for HBM price increases is high
- NVIDIA evaluating **4 HBM configs for Rubin Ultra** due to 12-Hi HBM4e yield/supply uncertainty: 12-Hi HBM4e (original), 8-Hi HBM4e, 12-Hi HBM4, 8-Hi HBM4

## 新增知識 / New Knowledge Added

- **HBM 定價動態結構性轉變**：DDR5 現貨市場飆漲與 HBM 年度合約鎖定的非同步性，導致 2026 年出現 HBM 獲利率低於 DDR5 的首次翻轉——這強化了 2027 年 HBM 大幅漲價的基礎
- **NVIDIA 系統毛利緩衝確認**：HBM4 成本翻倍仍可維持 75–80% GPU 系統毛利——代表 NVIDIA 有充分空間接受供應商更高 HBM 報價，記憶體廠談判槓桿大幅提升
- **Rubin Ultra 多配置評估**：12-Hi HBM4e 並非唯一選項，HBM4 8-Hi 備案正被評估——對 SK Hynix/Samsung HBM4 出貨量預測影響重大
