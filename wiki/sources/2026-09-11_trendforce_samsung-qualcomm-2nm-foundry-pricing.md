# TrendForce: Samsung-Qualcomm 2nm Deal May Slip to 2027; Foundry Gains Pricing Power

## 核心主張 / Key Claims

- Samsung 2nm (GAA) yield now **>70%** (up from <50% early 2026); process technically stabilized
- Qualcomm–Samsung 2nm deal may slip to 2027 due to pricing disagreement, not yield issues
- Samsung Foundry raised 4nm/5nm/8nm prices **10–15%** in July 2026 on tightened TSMC capacity
- First Taylor fab reportedly **fully booked** before opening: Tesla AI5/AI6, Broadcom comms, Arm AI chips
- Samsung Foundry ranked 2nd globally in Q2'26 foundry revenue (5.9% market share)
