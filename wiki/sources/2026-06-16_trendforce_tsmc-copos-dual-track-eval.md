# TSMC CoPoS Pilot Line Dual-Track Equipment Evaluation

## 核心主張 / Key Claims

- TSMC's Longtan CoPoS pilot line is operating two parallel equipment evaluation tracks simultaneously: one with global leaders (Applied Materials, TEL, SCREEN, DISCO, KLA, Camtek), one with Taiwanese local suppliers (Gudeng, Mirle, Scientech, VisEra, GPM, GPTC, Utechzone)
- Panel size confirmed: **310×310 mm** at pilot stage
- Mini production line at **VisEra** planned for 2026
- Small-volume trial production: **2027**; mass production: **2028–2029**
- Samsung is pursuing **415×510 mm** format panels (different specification from TSMC)
