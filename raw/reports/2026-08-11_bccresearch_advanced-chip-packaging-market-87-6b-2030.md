## Key Findings

- **Primary growth driver:** Surging AI and HPC workloads demanding faster data throughput, lower latency, higher interconnect density
- **Asia-Pacific** dominant region at $19.9B (2024): Taiwan, South Korea, Japan, China OSAT + foundry concentration
- **HBM packaging** = critical investment category (SK Hynix $12.9B Cheongju facility cited)
- **Chiplet architecture** amplifying packaging complexity — sub-5µm interconnect precision demanded
- **Packaging transition:** commodity → high-value, IP-intensive discipline

## Technology Coverage
Key technologies mentioned:
- 2.5D/3D: CoWoS, SoIC, TSV-based approaches
- Hybrid bonding (sub-nanometer accuracy)
- Fan-Out Panel-Level Packaging (FOPLP)
- Co-packaged optics (CPO) / silicon photonics
- Embedded die packaging (automotive SDV)

## Notable Item
- NVIDIA-Intel collaboration on NVLink-integrated x86 CPUs with NVIDIA RTX GPU chiplets cited as signal of packaging's central role in next-gen AI system design

## Market Drivers
1. AI compute arms race (model complexity → chiplet/heterogeneous packaging)
2. Government industrial policy (US CHIPS Act, EU CHIPS Act)
3. 5G infrastructure expansion
4. Automotive electronics (software-defined vehicle architectures)

## Risk Factors
- Capital intensity (multibillion-dollar facilities)
- Long customer qualification cycles
- Cyclical semiconductor demand
- Geopolitical uncertainty (US tariffs up to 100% on excess chip imports, export controls)
