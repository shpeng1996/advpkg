# 重組晶圓式晶片對晶圓混合接合 / Reconstituted-wafer C2W hybrid bonding（北京芯力）

## 英文標題
Chip-to-wafer hybrid bonding method and semiconductor device

## 摘要 / Abstract（OPS 原文）
The invention provides a chip-to-wafer hybrid bonding method and a semiconductor device, and the method comprises the steps: cutting an incoming wafer which is represented by indium gallium arsenic and serves as a second-generation semiconductor wafer into a plurality of single chips, and then rearranging the single chips on a carrier silicon wafer to form a recombined indium gallium arsenic chip + wafer; the method comprises the following steps: taking a recombined indium gallium arsenic chip and a wafer as a whole, carrying out wafer level alignment hybrid bonding on the whole and a readout circuit wafer, and then carrying out an annealing process on stacked wafers by using an annealing furnace to enhance the overall bonding strength; removing the carrier wafer on the back surface of the indium gallium arsenic chip through a de-bonding process; thinning the gallium arsenide sensor chip to form an ultrathin film layer, so that visible light can penetrate through the lnP layer to reach the lnGaAs layer; and the electrical property of the stacked core particles is led out from the back surface of the reading circuit through a partial filling type final conductive hole/welding pad TSV manufacturing process, so that the wafer-level indium gallium arsenic sensor chip is formed. According to the invention, the chip yield, the design flexibility and the material diversity can be improved.

## 為何對本 wiki 重要 / Why this matters
**北京芯力於 2026-02 公開之本件專利顯示**，D2W／C2W 的速度與良率兩難有**第三條處置路徑**：先把 InGaAs 晶粒切單、**重排至載體矽晶圓形成「重組晶圓」**，再以**晶圓級對準**一次完成與讀出電路晶圓的混合接合，接合後退火、解接合移除載體、薄化、最後以**部分填充型 via-last TSV** 自讀出電路背面引出電性。即：**以重組換取「W2W 的對準方式 + D2W 的挑片能力」**。

➜ 📌 此與既載 **imec 的 collective D2W（~30 nm SiCN 介電接合）** 屬**同一手法的兩個落點**：兩者皆把「逐顆對準」轉為「一次晶圓級對準」。本件並把該手法用於**化合物半導體（InGaAs/InP）異質整合**，為本 wiki 首見。觸及 [[technologies/hybrid-bonding]]、[[technologies/tsv]]。

➜ 📌 與本輪 In Practise 訪談（D2W 須 7,800 die／wafer、1,000–2,000 die/hr，速度提高即對準變差）構成**問題與解法的對照**：若改以重組晶圓一次對準，則「每顆晶粒的對準時間」不再是吞吐瓶頸。⚠ 本 wiki 推論；專利未提吞吐量。

⚠ **保留措辭**：請求項**零量化值**（無對準精度、退火溫度、薄化厚度、良率數字），僅稱可提升「晶片良率、設計靈活性與材料多樣性」。不得作為已量產能力引用。
