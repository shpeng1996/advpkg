# Amkor：凸出於介電層外表面且具「輪廓化側壁」的凸墊

## 英文標題
SEMICONDUCTOR DEVICES AND METHODS OF MANUFACTURING SEMICONDUCTOR DEVICES

## 摘要（OPS 原文）
In one example, an electronic device comprises an electronic component including a contact pad. A first dielectric structure can be disposed over the electronic component and can define an opening that exposes the contact pad from the first dielectric structure. A conductive structure can be disposed over the first dielectric structure and coupled to the contact pad. A second dielectric structure can be disposed over the conductive structure and coupled to the contact pad. The second dielectric structure can comprise an outer side. A protruding pad of the conductive structure can include a contoured sidewall protruding from the outer side of the second dielectric structure.

## 為何對 wiki 重要（2–4 句）

1. 請求項把**墊的側壁形貌（contoured sidewall）**與**凸出於介電層外表面**一併寫成排他權 —— 即把「墊的三維輪廓」當作可請求的設計參數，而非製程結果。觸及 [[entities/amkor]] 與 RDL／互連可靠度相關頁。
2. ⭐ 這與既載橫向論述「**銅的微結構正自『製程結果』變成『可請求的設計參數』**」（JCET 晶粒尺寸梯度／Absolics 上下 RDL 晶粒長寬比 0.85–0.99）**同向但換了層級**：前者請求的是**晶粒微結構**，本件請求的是**墊的巨觀側壁輪廓**。可記為該論述的第三個落點，且是第一個落在幾何輪廓而非冶金學上者。
3. ⚠ **無量化值**：無凸出高度、無側壁角度、無墊徑、無與既載混合接合 Cu recess（1–5 nm 需求／5–25 nm 實績）的任何對應 ⇒ 本件屬**凸出**方向，與混合接合所要求的**凹陷／齊平**方向相反，**不得與 Cu recess 數字混用**。
4. ⚠ 依專利訊號原則，本件不代表 Amkor 已量產此墊形貌。
