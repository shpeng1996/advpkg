# CN121335572A — Amkor：RDL 覆蓋鈍化層，保護層覆蓋元件之**橫向側**

## 英文題名

Electronic device and method of manufacturing same

## 摘要（英文，OPS 原文）

An electronic device includes an electronic component including a first side, a second side opposite the first side, **a lateral side connecting the first side to the second side**, a bond pad adjacent to the first side, and a passivation layer over the first side and including an opening exposing the bond pad. A redistribution structure is over the passivation layer and the bonding pad. The redistribution structure includes a conductive structure and a dielectric structure coupled to the bond pad. The conductive structure includes an outer terminal. External interconnects are coupled to the outside terminals, and **a protective layer covers the lateral sides of the electronic component**.

## 為何對本 wiki 重要 / Why this matters

1. **觸及 [[technologies/rdl]] 與 [[entities/amkor]]**：請求項把「RDL 結構置於鈍化層之上」與「保護層覆蓋元件橫向側」寫在同一件裡 —— 這是把**周界保護**納入扇出／WLCSP 基本結構的寫法。
2. 本 wiki 既載「**周界／邊緣是獨立的失效面向**」已有五個落點（Silicon Box 密封環、Lam bevel 管理、Deca 模封免孔橋、EVG 兩段式解接合、海時高邊緣電流屏蔽格柵）。本件為**第六個落點，且首次落在 OSAT 的標準扇出封裝結構上**（既有落點偏設備／面板電鍍／橋接載體）。
3. ⚠ **本件零量化值**：無保護層厚度、無覆蓋高度、無可靠度數據，亦未指明應用產品線。依專利訊號原則僅列追蹤，不改既有論述。
4. ⚠ 本件為 Amkor 新加坡控股實體之案件，與既載 Amkor 江陰／韓國團隊之產能定位無法由本件連結。
