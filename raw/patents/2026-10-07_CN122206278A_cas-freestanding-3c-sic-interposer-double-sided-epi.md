# 中科院半導體所：雙面同時生長 3C-SiC、再濕蝕刻掉矽 —— 自立式碳化矽中介層，厚度 100–200 µm

## 英文摘要（原文）

> The invention belongs to the technical field of semiconductors, and particularly relates to a preparation method of a silicon carbide interposer. The preparation method of the silicon carbide interposer comprises the following steps: carbonizing the upper and lower surfaces of a silicon wafer to form silicon carbide buffer layers on the upper and lower surfaces of the silicon wafer; depositing and growing **3C-SiC layers on the upper surface and the lower surface** of the silicon wafer coated with the silicon carbide buffer layer to obtain a composite wafer; and removing the silicon carbide layer wrapping the edge of the silicon wafer in the composite wafer, exposing the side edge of the silicon wafer in the composite wafer, and **removing the silicon wafer in the composite wafer in a wet etching mode** to obtain the silicon carbide intermediate layer. According to the preparation method of the silicon carbide intermediate layer, a scheme of simultaneously growing 3C-SiC layers on two sides is adopted, **symmetrical stress of equal-thickness epitaxial layers on the two sides is mutually counteracted**, so that a composite wafer is always kept flat in the high-temperature growth and cooling process, the limit of thick film growth is broken through, the warping problem in 3C-SiC heteroepitaxy is solved, and the yield of the silicon carbide intermediate layer is improved. And the preparation of the **high-quality thick film of 100-200 microns** becomes possible.
