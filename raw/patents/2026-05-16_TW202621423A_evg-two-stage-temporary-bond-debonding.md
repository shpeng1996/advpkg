# Method for temporarily bonding and releasing a product substrate and a carrier substrate, and device for such a method

## 摘要 / Abstract (as returned by EPO OPS search/biblio)

Method for temporarily bonding a product substrate (1) and a carrier substrate (2), comprising: providing the product substrate (1) and the carrier substrate (2),bonding the product substrate (1) and the carrier substrate (2) to each other to form a bonding layer (4) between the product substrate (1) and the carrier substrate (2) in a bonded state, separating the product substrate (1) from the carrier substrate (2), wherein the product substrate (1) and the carrier substrate (2) are separated in a primary area (11) under gas evolution in a first separation step and are separated in a secondary area (12) surrounding the primary area (11), in particular closed and/or gas-tight, in a second separation step.

## **為何重要 / Why this matters**

1. **把 debonding 自「單一事件」拆成兩步、兩區。** 請求項把分離動作分為：**主區（primary area）以氣體生成（under gas evolution）分離**，再把**環繞主區、且特別為封閉／氣密之次區（secondary area）以第二步分離**。⇒ 既載「FOPLP 的真正瓶頸在被視為輔助步驟的 debonding」（2026-09-21 列為候選論述、2026-10-19 以 Kookmin 綜述部分結清但結論為「放大倍率無通用值」）在**排他權層**首次有對應的結構性解法，且其設計隱含一個本 wiki 此前未記載的物理前提：**周界與中央的分離條件不同**，故**不能一次剝離**。
2. **「周界是獨立變數」與既載多條論述同向**：Silicon Box 以 RDL 內建金屬密封環均勻化大面板電鍍、Lam 的斜角（bevel）管理、Deca 的垂直互連置於橋周界——**面板／晶圓的邊緣與周界正反覆出現為獨立失效面向**。本件是其在**暫時接合／解接合**上的版本。
3. ⚠ **全篇無量化值**（無分離力、無殘膠、無翹曲數字），依規範不得與既載翹曲數字比較。⚠ 公開號為 TW 案，family 93607590 同族另有 2026-05-16 之其他國別案，本輪未展開家族清單。
