# SEMICONDUCTOR PACKAGE AND METHOD OF MANUFACTURING THE SAME

**公開號** US20260282955A1 ｜ **family-id** 101296689 ｜ **公開日** 2026-09-17
**申請人** SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS CO LTD [KR]

## Abstract（原文）

A semiconductor package includes a bridge die; an encapsulant; a redistribution structure including redistribution patterns and an upper insulating layer; semiconductor chips disposed on the redistribution structure and electrically connected to the redistribution patterns; connection posts extending in a vertical direction in the encapsulant and electrically connected to the redistribution patterns; and a plurality of connection conductor structures below the bridge die and the connection posts, wherein the plurality of connection conductor structures include a first group of the plurality of connection conductor structures overlapping the bridge die, and a second group of the plurality of connection conductor structures overlapping the connection posts, wherein each of the first group of the plurality of connection conductor structures includes a connection pad; an upper connection solder in contact with and electrically connected to the bridge die on the connection pad; and a lower connection solder below the connection pad.

## 為何對本 wiki 重要（Why this matters）

1. **這是 Samsung 模封式橋接（molded bridge）的第四件，且是本 wiki 所見申請人之中最新的一件（2026-09-17，距收錄日僅 12 天）。** 既有三件為 US20260068717A1（2026-03-05，橋置 RDL 背面＋第二模封層）、US20260090411A1（2026-03-26，側面模封膜＋背面保護膜）、US20260144093A1（2026-05-21，玻璃核心中介層）。本件觸及 [[technologies/emib]] 與 [[entities/samsung]]。

2. **本件的差異點不在「橋的哪個表面」，而在「橋用什麼接上去」。** 前三件的下注粒度是橋的表面材料（第二模封層 vs 保護膜）；本件明確請求 **connection pad + upper connection solder + lower connection solder** 的三段式焊接連接結構，即橋以**焊料**而非 RDL 直接接觸與上方連接。⇒ **2026-09-28 記錄之候選論述「在模封式橋接中，橋的每一個表面是獨立的設計變數」應擴充為「橋的每個表面**與其接合方式**都是獨立設計變數」。**

3. **connection post 與 bridge die 共用同一層 connection conductor 但分為兩組（first group 覆蓋橋、second group 覆蓋 post）**，顯示 Samsung 把「橋區」與「垂直貫穿區」的連接結構在同一製程層內分開設計。此與 [[technologies/rdl]] 中記載之「粗快／細慢混合流程」屬同型態，但落在連接結構而非圖案化。

⚠ **本件全篇無任何量化值**（無節距、無厚度、無焊料尺寸）。依 2026-09-27／28 之觀察，專利軌以定性請求項為主的趨勢在本件延續。
