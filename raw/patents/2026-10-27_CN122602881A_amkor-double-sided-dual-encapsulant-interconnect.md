# Amkor：基板雙面各掛元件、各自包封，外部互連穿出第二包封物

## 英文標題
SEMICONDUCTOR DEVICE AND METHOD OF MANUFACTURING SEMICONDUCTOR DEVICE

## 摘要（OPS 原文）
In one example, an electronic device includes a substrate including a first side, a second side opposite the first side, a dielectric structure, and a conductive structure; a first electronic component coupled to the first side of the substrate and coupled to the conductive structure; a second electronic component coupled to the second side of the substrate and coupled to the conductive structure; a first encapsulant on the first side of the substrate and covering the first electronic component; a second encapsulant on the second side of the substrate and covering the second electronic component; a first interconnect structure in the second encapsulant and coupled to the conductive structure; and an external interconnect coupled to the first interconnect structure, where the external interconnect is external to the second encapsulant.

## 為何對 wiki 重要（2–4 句）

1. 本件把元件掛在**基板的兩面**並**各自包封**，外部互連則**穿過第二包封物**引出。相當於用「雙面掛載」取代「單面加高堆疊」來換取封裝內的元件密度。觸及 [[entities/amkor]]。
2. ⭐ **「雙面化」是 Amkor 的連續布局**：本 wiki 已載同申請人 **TW202607900A（fam 98776443, 2026-02-16）** —— RDL 基板兩面各以**混合接合**接一枚元件，且第二接合界面落在第一接合界面的 footprint 內。本件與該件構成**同一手法的兩個精緻度層級（傳統銲接雙面 vs 混合接合雙面）** ⇒ 可記為「雙面基板掛載」在 Amkor 內部的兩段式路線，而非單一個案。
3. ⚠ **無量化值**，且雙面包封的**翹曲**代價（兩側 EMC 固化收縮不對稱）摘要完全未述 —— 依既載「翹曲成因須分可逆（CTE）與不可逆（化學固化收縮）」，本件正是不可逆項的高風險構型，列為追蹤點。
4. ⚠ CPC 含 H10P72（功率元件）⇒ **不得推論本件瞄準 AI／HPC 封裝**。
