# METHOD AND APPARATUS FOR MITIGATING CRACKS IN A GLASS CORE

**Applicant**：INTEL CORP
**Publication**：JP2026077559A（2026-05-13）｜**family-id** 94481277
**IPC/CPC**：H05K1/181、H10W20/033、H10W42/121、H10W70/095、H10W70/635、H10B80/00、H10P72/74

## Abstract（英文，OPS 原文）

To provide a method and an apparatus for reducing cracks in a glass core. SOLUTION: The first package substrate 200 includes a glass core 202 having openings 204a, 204b, 204c extending between a first surface 206 and a second surface 208. Within the opening, conductive materials 210a, 210b, and 210c are provided to define a through glass via (TGV). **The conductive material is spaced apart from the glass core by voids 212a, 212b, 212c in a radial direction relative to the longitudinal axes 214a, 214b, 214c of the TGV.** This provides space within the opening of the glass core to allow for expansion and/or contraction of the conductive material. Thus, the TGV can expand into the void with little to no force being exerted on the inner wall of the glass core, protecting the glass core from the effects of potentially damaging cracks under pressure.
