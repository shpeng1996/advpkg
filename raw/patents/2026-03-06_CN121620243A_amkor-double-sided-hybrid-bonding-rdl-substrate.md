## 摘要 / Abstract (as returned by EPO OPS search/biblio)

An electronic device and a method of manufacturing the electronic device. In one example, an apparatus includes a redistribution layer (RDL) substrate including a first side and a second side opposite the first side. A wafer assembly may be coupled to the first side of the RDL substrate. A first bonding interface may be disposed between the wafer assembly and the RDL substrate. The first bonding interface may be provided by a first hybrid bonding. An electronic component may be coupled to the second side of the RDL substrate. A second bonding interface may be disposed between the electronic component and the RDL substrate. The second bonding interface may be within the footprint of the first bonding interface and may be provided by a second hybrid bonding. A vertical interconnect may be disposed transversely to a sidewall of the electronic component. The vertical interconnect may be coupled to the RDL substrate. Other examples and related methods are also disclosed herein.
