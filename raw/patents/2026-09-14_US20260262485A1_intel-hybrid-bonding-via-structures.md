# VIA STRUCTURES FOR HYBRID BONDING ARCHITECTURES

**Publication Number**: US20260262485A1
**Family ID**: 100905151
**Applicant**: Intel Corp [US]
**Published**: 2026-09-03

## Abstract (from EPO OPS)

Via structures for hybrid bonding architectures, and methods for making same. The structures include a die with a plurality of conductive contacts arranged at a pitch of less than about 4 micrometers (µm), forming a high-density hybrid bonding interface for die-to-die and die-to-wafer stacking applications.

## IPC/CPC Classes
- H10W (Heterojunction transistors / integrated circuits with heterojunctions)

## Wiki Significance

This Intel patent (published 2026-09-03) discloses via structures for hybrid bonding with contact pitch below 4 µm, signaling Intel's active R&D push below the 5 µm pitch threshold. This places Intel in direct competition with TSMC's SoIC-X (approaching 4 µm pitch roadmap) and imec's 1 µm pitch research targets. The patent reinforces Intel's ECTC 2026 disclosures on ultra-low-temperature D2W bonding and EV Group 100% overlay accuracy.
