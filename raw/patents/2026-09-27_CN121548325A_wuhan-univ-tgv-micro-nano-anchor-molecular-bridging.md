# High-density packaged TGV gradient material filling interconnection structure

**Publication**: CN121548325A ｜ **Family**: 98749508 ｜ **Published**: 2026-02-17
**Applicant**: 武漢大學 Wuhan University ｜ **First inventor**: Liu Sheng（劉勝）

## 摘要 / Abstract (English, from OPS biblio)

> The TGV gradient material filling interconnection structure comprises a glass through hole located in a glass substrate, and the glass through hole is filled with a conductive metal body. A **micro-nano anchor structure layer, a molecular bridging layer and a conductive metal seed layer** are sequentially arranged between the inner wall of the glass through hole and the conductive metal main body, and a metal bridging structure is arranged in the middle of the interior of the glass through hole; the micro-nano anchor structure layer is located on the surface of the inner wall of the glass through hole and is of a **concave-convex dot matrix arrangement structure**. ... the copper-glass interface bonding strength is remarkably improved, and **void-free electroplating filling of the TGV through hole with the depth-diameter ratio larger than or equal to 10:1** is achieved. The glass bridge is embedded in the glass substrate, **ultra-high-density interconnection with local interconnection line width/line distance smaller than or equal to 1 µm** is achieved [satisfying the 2.5D integration requirement of **16 or more silicon chiplets** — from the Chinese abstract].

## 為何對本 wiki 重要 / Why this matters

- ⭐⭐⭐ **2026-09-26 建立的「TGV 的失效在界面與孔緣，不在材料本體」論述，本輪首次取得一件以該論述為整體設計前提的排他權文件。** 既有四條 TGV 金屬化路線（Corning 的 Ti/Cu + 羥基富化 + 矽烷官能化 + 無電鍍、Intel 的有機種子層／部分 liner／底部向上電鍍、奧野的金屬氧化物黏結層、E&R 的雙載板流程軸）全部改動**材料或流程順序**；**本件改動的是孔壁的「幾何」** —— 以微納錨釘做機械互鎖，再以分子橋連層做化學接合。➜ **應記為第五條路線，且其自由度是「孔壁形貌」，與既有四條互不重疊。**
- ⭐⭐ **「粗糙度是雙面刃」的第四個技術域。** 本 wiki 既有記述：混合接合要 Ra <0.1–0.2 nm（越平越好）、TGV 側壁 25 nm–1.257 µm、LPKF 腔體粗糙度 ±30 nm。**本件把 TGV 孔壁的粗糙度當成刻意追求的正向變數**（錨釘＝有意造形貌）➜ 與 2026-09-18「混合接合的最佳粗糙度是否真的非零」在**兩個相反的技術域同時出現同一個問題形式**。
- ⭐⭐ **「玻璃橋埋在玻璃基板中」是本 wiki 首見的同質橋方案。** 同日五件專利中，Intel 四種、Samsung 一種、珠海天成一種的橋載體分別是玻璃腔體／玻璃貼片／玻璃中介層／有機層腔體／TGV 之上／矽轉接板；**本件是「玻璃橋 in 玻璃」** ➜ 「橋要埋進什麼材料裡」這條軸新增一個同質解，並使 L/S ≤1 µm 首次與玻璃橋綁在一起。
- ⚠⚠ **學術機構單一申請案，無量產佐證**；「無空洞」與「≤1 µm」皆為請求項主張，**未附量測分布、良率或重複性數據**（依 2026-09-21 規範標 ⚠）。「≥16 顆芯粒」為應用宣稱，非實作。
