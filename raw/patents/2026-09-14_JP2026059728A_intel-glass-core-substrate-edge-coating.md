# EDGE COATING OF FRAMED GLASS CORE SUBSTRATES

**Publication Number**: JP2026059728A
**Family ID**: 98977174
**Applicant**: Intel Corp
**Published**: 2026-04-07

## Abstract (from EPO OPS)

To provide a glass core substrate having necessary mechanical robustness and compatibility with a manufacturing and assembling process of a semiconductor package. The device includes a package substrate featuring a glass core with an edge coating applied to the framed glass to prevent edge-initiated fractures during handling and assembly.

## IPC/CPC Classes
- H10W (Integrated circuits)
- H05K (Printed circuits; casings)

## Wiki Significance

Intel's April 2026 patent addresses a key glass substrate manufacturing challenge: edge fracture during handling. Edge coating of framed glass core substrates is a critical reliability enabler for TGV (Through-Glass Via) substrates, consistent with Intel's ECTC 2026 disclosure of zero-failure TGV copper fill.
