# 北儀半導體：以「雙應力緩衝障壁 + 四段燒結 + 梯度冷卻」壓低 SiC 功率模組基板翹曲

## 摘要 / Abstract (EPO OPS, verbatim)
The invention discloses a low-buckling-deformation SiC power module interconnection structure, a SiC power module and a preparation method of the SiC power module, and relates to the field of semiconductor preparation. The preparation method of the SiC power module with low buckling deformation comprises the steps of substrate and buffer layer pretreatment and assembly, chip mounting and interconnection layer sintering, electrode connection and stress isolation, and packaging integration and stabilization treatment. According to the preparation method of the low-buckling-deformation SiC power module, a pretreatment, sintering, welding and packaging whole-process cooperative stress control system is constructed, a titanium-copper composite buffer layer and an invar buffer sheet form a dual stress buffer barrier, four-section sintering and gradient cooling accurately release thermal stress, and the stress matching design of an electrode connection and packaging process is adopted. Thermal stress accumulation in each link is effectively inhibited, buckling deformation of the substrate is remarkably reduced, meanwhile, the metallurgical bonding reliability of the SiC power chip and the copper-clad ceramic substrate is guaranteed, and the heat dissipation efficiency and the long-term working stability of the module are greatly improved.

## 為何對本 wiki 重要 / Why this matters
1. ⭐⭐⭐ **翹曲的處置手法首次出現「以第二種材料的剛性去對抗，而非以製程參數去避免」。** 既載本 wiki 之翹曲處置集中在 FOPLP 側（載板材料選擇、debonding 階段、die shift 之四類補償對象），全部是**幾何或製程層**的補償；本件以**鈦銅複合緩衝層 + 因瓦（invar）緩衝片**組成「雙應力緩衝障壁」，即以**低 CTE 金屬插層**吸收應力 ⇒ 📌 **翹曲對策此後記為三類：改製程參數／改載體幾何／⭐ 插入低 CTE 緩衝層。**
2. ⭐⭐ **「四段燒結 + 梯度冷卻」是本 wiki 第一個把燒結拆成多段升降溫區段的排他權落點**，與本輪論文軌 TU Delft 之大面積燒結（LAS）回顧所列三大難題（有機黏結劑燒除與排氣／模組翹曲／銅氧化）中的**第二項**正面對應 ⇒ **專利給手法、論文給問題清單，且兩者為互不相關的申請人／機構。**
3. ⚠ **零量化值**：無翹曲量（µm）、無燒結溫度與時間、無 CTE 值、無剪切強度。「remarkably reduced」「greatly improved」為申請人自述，無對照組。⇒ 不得與本 wiki 既載任何翹曲數字（如 FOPLP 之翹曲峰值）並列或相減。
4. ⚠ **元件域為 SiC 功率模組，非 AI 加速器封裝。** 其效力範圍僅及於「翹曲對策的手法分類」與「燒結分段的存在」，不得套用於 HBM／chiplet 之接合預算或熱預算。
