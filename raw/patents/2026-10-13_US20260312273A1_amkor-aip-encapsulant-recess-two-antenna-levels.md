# Amkor：封膠體外側凹穴使天線圖案分佈在兩個不同高度

## 摘要 / Abstract (EPO OPS, verbatim)
In one example, an electronic device includes a substrate including a first side and a second side. An antenna structure includes antenna feed structures connected to the first side and antenna patterns. An encapsulant covers the antenna feed structures and the first side and includes an outer side and a recess extending inward from the outer side. The recess includes a lower side below the outer side. The antenna feed structures includes first and second antenna feed structures. The antenna patterns include a first antenna pattern adjacent to the lower side and connected to the first antenna feed structure and a second antenna pattern adjacent to the outer side and coupled to the second antenna feed structure. Electronic components are connected to the substrate at the second side and are coupled to the antenna feed structures. Other examples and related methods are also disclosed herein.

## 為何對本 wiki 重要 / Why this matters
1. ⭐⭐⭐ **「封膠體的凹穴」首次被當成一個可指派的幾何資源，且指派對象是天線的高度。** 第一天線圖案貼著凹穴底面、第二天線圖案貼著封膠外側面 ⇒ 兩組輻射體被刻意放在**兩個不同的 z 高度**，而兩者各自有獨立的饋入結構。既載本 wiki 關於「封裝的面正在成為被分配的資源」之論述一向以**晶粒的面**（連接／散熱／貫穿／光路）為落點；本件把同一型態移到**封膠體自身的外表面與其凹穴**。
2. ⭐⭐ **這是本 wiki 第一件由 OSAT 提出的 AiP 結構排他權。** 既載 AiP 側的檢索結果（EPO `ti,ab="antenna in package"` 僅 7 件）申請人為 POSTECH、Doosan、ITRI、LG Innotek、FujiFilm —— 皆為學研、材料或模組商；Amkor 件並未以「antenna-in-package」為標題用語（其標題為一貫的 ELECTRONIC DEVICES…），⇒ 📌 **與既載規範（44）（48）同向之第三個具體化：以主題片語檢索會系統性漏掉 OSAT 的佈局，因為 OSAT 的標題策略是刻意泛化的。**
3. ⚠ **零量化值**：無頻段、無凹穴深度、無天線間距、無增益。不得與本輪論文軌所得之 AiP 量測值（28 GHz／83–110 GHz／6.9–10.2 dBi）並列比較。
4. ⚠ **為專利訊號而非量產能力**；亦未揭露目標應用（手機、雷達或 CPE 皆有可能）。
