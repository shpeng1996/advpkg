# Method of manufacturing semiconductor device and fan-out panel level package

**Applicant**：SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS CO LTD

## Abstract（英文，OPS 原文）

A method of manufacturing a semiconductor device and a fan-out panel level package are provided. A method of manufacturing a semiconductor device may include **forming a semiconductor sub-package by performing a fan-out wafer level package process**; **singulating the semiconductor sub-package from the at least one wafer**; and **reconstructing the semiconductor sub-package into a fan-out panel level package (FOPLP)**.
