# SMALL VIA DIAMETER TGV WITH ADHESION LAYER

## 摘要 / Abstract（原文重點）
A through-glass via (TGV) structure and method of forming the TGV structure are described. The TGV structure includes a glass substrate with multiple vias, an **adhesion layer of titanium and copper** on the via sidewalls, a copper seed layer on the adhesion layer, and copper filling the vias. Fabrication involves depositing the adhesion layer by **physical vapor deposition**, treating the substrate in an **acid solution to enrich hydroxyl groups**, functionalizing the substrate with a **silane solution**, forming a copper seed layer by **electroless deposition**, and filling the vias with copper. The process may include **annealing before CMP**.
