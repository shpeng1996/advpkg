# Package substrate with components included in cavity of glass core

**Applicant**：INTEL CORP
**Publication**：CN122249079A（2026-06-19）｜**family-id** 95339651
**IPC/CPC**：H10W20/20、H10W20/42、H10W20/435、H10W44/601、H10W70/05、H10W70/095、H10W70/611、H10W70/614

## Abstract（英文，OPS 原文）

A packaging substrate having components included in a cavity of a glass core is disclosed. An example device includes: **a glass layer having an opening between opposing first and second surfaces** of the glass layer; **an electronic component within the opening**; **a dielectric material within the opening and between the electronic component and a sidewall of the opening**; and **a through-glass via comprising a conductive material extending through the glass layer**.
