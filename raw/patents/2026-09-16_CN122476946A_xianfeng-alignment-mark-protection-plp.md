# Alignment mark protection for panel-level packaging (Shanghai Xianfeng)

**Publication**: CN122476946A · **Family**: 100638797 · **Published**: 2026-07-28
**Applicant**: Shanghai Xianfeng Technology Co., Ltd.

## Abstract (EPO OPS, machine translation as published)

> The invention discloses an alignment mark protection method and a panel-level packaging structure... According to the protection method of the alignment mark, the **functional protection layer with anti-oxidation and optical enhancement dual functions** is formed on the metal alignment mark, oxygen can be effectively isolated in the subsequent **heating chip bonding process**, oxidation discoloration of the metal mark is restrained, meanwhile, the **optical contrast ratio and the marginal definition** of the mark are improved... so that the **chip bonding search failure rate and the alignment offset correction frequency** caused by mark oxidation are remarkably reduced, the product scrap risk is reduced, and the **overall yield of panel-level packaging is improved**.

## The claim in plain terms

Metal fiducial marks on a panel oxidise during the thermal steps of die bonding; a discoloured, low-contrast mark causes the bonder's vision system to fail its search or to mis-register. The fix is a dual-purpose overcoat on the fiducial: an **oxygen barrier** that also acts as an **optical contrast enhancer**.
