# Photoelectric hybrid redistribution structure (ASE)

**Publication**: CN224583735U (utility model) · **Family**: 100681519 · **Published**: 2026-07-31
**Applicant**: Advanced Semiconductor Engineering (ASE)

## Abstract (EPO OPS, machine translation as published)

> The utility model provides a photoelectric hybrid rewiring structure, and the structure comprises an electronic element which comprises an optical welding pad and an electrical welding pad. The light guide material is arranged above the optical welding pad; and the top conductive material is arranged above the light guide material and is in contact with the light guide material, and the top conductive material is used as a reflection interface of the light guide material. Therefore, the photoconductive material and the conductive material are overlapped, the photoelectric interconnection distance is shortened, the advantages of high light transmission speed and low power consumption are utilised, the signal transmission efficiency is improved, and more I/O (Input/Output) interfaces are provided by utilising a re-wiring structure. And meanwhile, product miniaturization is facilitated.

## Structural claim in plain terms

An RDL stack that carries **optical pads and electrical pads side by side**. Above each optical pad sits a **light-guide (waveguide) material**; above the light-guide sits the **top metal**, which does double duty as the **reflective interface** confining the optical mode. Optical and electrical routing are therefore *vertically co-stacked in the same redistribution layer* rather than being placed in separate tiers.

## 為何重要 / Why this matters to the wiki

- Touches [[technologies/copackaged-optics]] and [[entities/ase-group]]. ASE is best known in CPO discussion as an assembly partner, not as an owner of optical-RDL structure IP; this is a structural claim on the interconnect layer itself.
- The claim's economic logic is metal reuse: by making the existing top conductive layer serve as the waveguide's reflector, the optical layer adds **no extra mask tier** — an OSAT-style cost argument rather than a foundry-style performance argument, and a contrast with TSMC's COUPE/photonic-engine approach (grating couplers + microlenses, CN122260581A, collected 2026-09-14).
- Utility model (實用新型), not an invention patent: examination is formal only, so this is a **positioning signal**, not evidence of a qualified process.
