# METHOD FOR PRODUCING A SURFACE PROFILE ON A SUBSTRATE

**申請人**：EV Group E. Thallner GmbH（奧地利）
**發明人**：Rimböck Johanna、Vsetecka Lisa
**分類**：**G03F7/0002**（壓印微影／imprint lithography）
**公開日**：2026-01-08

## 摘要（OPS 原文）

> A method for producing a surface profile on a substrate (3), in particular in the context of producing an electrical and/or optical component, comprising: providing the substrate (3); positioning a first quantity (101) of an embossing material on the substrate (3); positioning a second quantity (102) of an embossing material or of a further embossing material on the substrate (3); forming the surface profile by means of an embossing die element (4) or an exposure masking, which acts on the embossing material and/or the further embossing material, wherein a mixing of the first quantity (101) with the second quantity (102) is controlled by the positioning of the first quantity (101) and the second quantity (102).
