# BUILD UP BONDING LAYER PROCESS AND STRUCTURE FOR LOW TEMPERATURE BONDING

## 摘要 / Abstract (as returned by EPO OPS search/biblio)

[0000]    Disclosed herein are methods of forming a microelectronic component. In some embodiments, the methods include providing a substrate having a first surface, forming a first metal feature on the first surface, forming a second metal feature on the first metal feature, forming a dielectric layer over the substrate such that the dielectric layer directly contacts sidewalls of the first and second metal features, and planarizing the dielectric layer to form a second surface for hybrid bonding. After planarizing the dielectric layer, the second metal feature is exposed at the second surface.

## **為何重要 / Why this matters**

1. **為既載「Adeia EP4709132A2 機制詮釋」空缺提供同方向的第二件證據。** 2026-09-21 本 wiki 列管一項**自承為推論**的讀法：「以阻障層作研磨停止層 = 把奈米級高度控制轉為薄膜厚度控制」。本件請求項的結構是**第一金屬特徵上再長第二金屬特徵**，介電層沉積後**平坦化**，使**第二金屬特徵**露出於接合面 ⇒ 接合面金屬高度由「**第二金屬特徵的成長厚度**」決定，而非由 CMP 的去除量決定。**此即前述推論所描述的同一手法，但由另一件 Adeia 專利以不同結構實作。** ⚠ 摘要未提阻障層或研磨停止層，故原推論仍不得視為已證實，僅升格為「同一申請人在兩件專利上採同一設計哲學」。
2. **標題明示 low temperature bonding** ⇒ 與既載低溫路線（IBM/RPI 250 °C CuO 門檻、上海大學濕式還原、Atotech 預退火窗）同一問題域；Adeia 的解法不在化學或溫度，而在**把高度控制移到沉積端**。
3. 發明人 FOUNTAIN JR GAIUS GILLMAN 為 Adeia（原 Invensas/Ziptronix）混合接合基礎專利之長期發明人，與既載 Cyprian Emeka Uzoh 並列。
