# MICROELECTRONIC ASSEMBLIES INCLUDING A PARTIAL LINER IN THROUGH-GLASS VIAS

## 摘要 / Abstract（原文重點）
Disclosed herein are microelectronic assemblies and related devices and methods for **alleviating stresses in through-glass vias by providing a partial liner**. A microelectronic assembly may include a glass core having a first and an opposing second surface; a TGV extending between the two surfaces, wherein the TGV includes a conductive material and has a first height; and **a liner material between the glass core and the conductive material of the TGV, wherein the liner extends from the second surface and has a second height that is less than the first height**.
