## Abstract

In one example, an electronic device includes an electronic component. A thermal-interface-material-flow (TIM-flow) layer is disposed over the electronic component and on a sidewall of the electronic component. A thermal interface material (TIM) is disposed on the TIM-flow layer and around the sidewall of the electronic component. A lid is disposed over the TIM. The TIM fills a volume defined between the lid and the electronic component. An inner side of a top plate of the lid can be substantially devoid of TIM outside a footprint of the electronic component. Other examples and related methods are also disclosed herein.

## 為何對本 wiki 重要

Amkor 的一件明確**熱路徑工程**請求項：導入一層專門引導 TIM 流動的「TIM-flow layer」，使 TIM 不只覆蓋晶片上表面、還包覆側壁，同時刻意讓 lid 頂板在晶片 footprint 之外「實質無 TIM」。其效果是把散熱面積從單一頂面擴張到頂面＋側壁，並避免 lid 外緣多餘 TIM 造成的翹曲與應力。

這與 wiki 已記錄的 Amkor CEO McCann 2026-09-08 預判「兩相冷卻為下一散熱轉型」形成有意義的對照：**在兩相冷卻之前，Amkor 仍在既有 lid+TIM 架構內榨取散熱餘裕**。可補入 `concepts/thermal-management.md` 與 `entities/amkor.md` 的專利訊號段落。本案為公開申請案，不代表已導入量產產品。
