# PACKAGE AND METHOD OF FORMING SAME — TSMC PHOTONIC ENGINE WITH GRATING COUPLERS

**Publication Number**: CN122260581A
**Family ID**: 100036235
**Applicant**: Taiwan Semiconductor Mfg Co Ltd
**Published**: 2026-06-23

## Abstract (from EPO OPS)

A method of forming a package includes forming a photon engine. Formation of the photonic engine includes receiving a photonic die including a plurality of grating couplers and a plurality of microlenses, and bonding electronic dies to the photonic die. The resulting package integrates photonic and electronic dies for co-packaged optics applications.
