# Optical engine and co-packaged optical device（光引擎與共封裝光學元件）

**申請人 / Applicant**：AU Optronics Corp（友達光電）
**公開日 / Publication date**：2026-05-15

## 摘要 / Abstract
The invention discloses an optical engine and a common packaging optical device. The optical engine comprises an optical substrate, an optical fiber assembly, a reflecting element and an optical circuit assembly. The optical substrate is provided with an upper surface and a lower surface which are opposite to each other, and a through hole penetrating through the upper surface and the lower surface of the optical substrate. The fiber assembly is disposed in the through hole. The reflecting element is arranged on an inner side wall of the through hole and surrounds the optical fiber assembly. The optical circuit assembly is arranged below the optical substrate, the optical circuit assembly comprises a circuit substrate and a light-emitting element, and the light-emitting element is arranged on the circuit substrate and corresponds to the through hole.
