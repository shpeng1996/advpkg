## 摘要 / Abstract（由 OpenAlex `abstract_inverted_index` 重建）

Advanced semiconductor packaging technologies, including high-bandwidth memory, chiplets, and three-dimensional stacked packages, have increased the importance of precisely singulating thin wafers into individual dies. This study reviews standards issued by the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) and Semiconductor Equipment and Materials International (SEMI) and analyzes the scope and evaluation procedures of draft IEC 63567-4, which addresses the dimensional accuracy of laser-diced dies. Existing standards separately cover terminology, wafer and die handling, wafer flatness, die strength, and die-attach integrity; however, they do not provide an integrated method for evaluating the dimensional and geometrical quality of cutting lines and sidewalls after dicing. IEC 63567-4 establishes evaluation methods for cutting-line straightness and positional deviation, sidewall taper and flatness, and chipping, together with requirements for recording measurement conditions and results in the test report. The proposed methods can improve the comparability of results obtained using different laser-dicing processes and equipment and can support process development, equipment performance evaluation, and quality agreements between semiconductor equipment suppliers and users.

## 關鍵內容 / Key content

- 對象：**IEC 63567-4 草案** —— 針對**雷射切割（laser dicing）後晶粒的尺寸精度**建立評估方法；本文同時回顧 **IEC 與 SEMI** 既有標準之範圍與程序。
- **既有標準的缺口（本文核心主張）**：現行標準**分別**涵蓋術語、晶圓與晶粒搬運、晶圓平坦度、**晶粒強度（die strength）**、晶粒貼附完整性，**但沒有一個整合性的方法去評估切割後「切割線與側壁」的尺寸與幾何品質**。
- IEC 63567-4 所訂的評估項：**切割線直線度（straightness）與位置偏差**、**側壁錐度（taper）與平坦度**、**崩缺（chipping）**，並**要求在測試報告中記錄量測條件與結果**。
- 宣稱效益：使不同雷射切割製程與設備所得結果**可相互比較**，並支撐製程開發、設備性能評估、以及**設備供應商與使用者之間的品質協議（quality agreements）**。
- 動機明載為先進封裝需求：**HBM、chiplet、3D 堆疊封裝**使「把薄晶圓精確單粒化」的重要性上升。

## 為何重要 / Why this matters

1. **「缺量測口徑時學界／標準組織先行」命題取得第二個獨立落點，且首次落在正式標準草案上。** 2026-10-19 本 wiki 把「標準缺位 ⇒ 私有方法先行」條件化為兩支：缺**能力**時廠商先行（CPO 晶圓級測試，ASE＋TSMC）、缺**量測口徑**時學界先行（FOPLP 翹曲度量，Kookmin 綜述呼籲最低報告要求）。本件屬後者，**但推進了一階**：不只是呼籲，而是**已進入 IEC 草案並明訂「須記錄量測條件」**——與 Kookmin 綜述所呼籲的「公開最低報告要求」是同一主張的制度化版本。
2. **把「同一名詞涵蓋多個獨立驗收項」從粗糙度擴及到「切割品質」。** 本 wiki 既載 TGV 側壁粗糙度（25 nm–1.257 µm）vs 混合接合 Ra（<0.1–0.2 nm）相差 2–4 個數量級，故跨頁引用須標技術域。本件顯示**切割**同樣不是單一量：直線度、位置偏差、側壁錐度、側壁平坦度、崩缺至少五項，**且此前無整合方法** ⇒ 📌 既載 Resonac「電漿切割道寬 1 µm」一類數字，應視為**單一項（道寬）**，不得當作切割品質的總結指標。
