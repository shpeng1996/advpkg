# SUSS MicroTec：噴墨列印作為先進封裝的加成式製程（IMAPS DPC 2026）

## 摘要 / Abstract（原文節錄）
> As advanced packaging evolves…, it continues to push the boundaries in terms of heterogeneous integration, package size and cost-efficiency. This development brings **conventional coating methods such as spin and spray coating to their physical and economic limits**. In contrast, **inkjet printing emerges as an additive technology**… offering distinct advantages… particularly in terms of **material efficiency, scalability and its ability to pattern surfaces without the need for subsequent exposure and development**.
> …recent advancements in inkjet printing of **photoresists, dielectrics and bonding materials, such as laser release and temporary bonding layers**… essential for 3D ICs, passivation or interconnect structures in FOWLP and SiP… The SiP approach leads to an evolution towards **ever larger packages that integrate the diverse chips and range over multiple exposure fields of a stepper.**

## 為何重要 / Why this matters
1. ⭐⭐⭐ **「免光罩／免曝顯」路線取得第三個落點，且第一次落在「材料塗佈」而非「圖案化」**。既載兩個落點：Deca Adaptive Patterning（免光罩圖案化）、Onto US20260186421A1（混合式微影＋直寫）。本件主張連**光阻、介電層、以及雷射解離層／暫時接合膠**都可噴墨成形。
   ⇒ 與既載「真正的瓶頸在被視為輔助步驟的那一步」呼應：**暫時接合／解離層正是該論述的既有實例（ERS debonding、Lam TurboCell），而這裡是把那一步的材料施加方式整個換掉。**
2. ⭐⭐ **旋塗／噴塗被設備商自己宣告「逼近物理與經濟極限」**，理由之一是**封裝尺寸已跨越多個步進機曝光場** ⇒ 與既載「面板 vs 晶圓」「光罩尺寸上限（CoWoS 14×）」同一組壓力的第三種表現。
3. ⚠ 會議簡報摘要，**無任何數值**（無解析度、膜厚、均勻度、產出率、材料利用率）；且為**設備商自述**（依規範 41 計為有利害關係之來源）。
