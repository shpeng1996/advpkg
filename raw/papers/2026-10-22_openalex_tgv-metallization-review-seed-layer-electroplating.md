# A Review of Research Progresses in Through-Glass Via (TGV) Metallization

**作者**：Tailong Shi、Lihong Chen、Qi Li、Yubing Luo、Shunyu Yao（東南大學 Southeast University）
**期刊**：Micromachines（MDPI）　**日期**：2026-10-22　**DOI**：10.3390/mi17111214　**被引**：0

## 摘要 / Abstract（原文）

Through-glass vias (TGVs), as key vertical interconnect structures in 2.5-D/3-D advanced packaging, offer low dielectric loss, low parasitic capacitance, and excellent high-frequency transmission performance. However, several challenges remain in TGV metallization, including (1) incontinuity of seed layers in high aspect ratio TGVs; (2) non-uniform distributions of copper-ion concentration and current density, leading to significant differences in copper deposition rates between the via interior and the via opening; (3) voids, seams, and double bridging caused by excessively rapid deposition at the via opening or poor control of the central bridging position; and (4) low filling rate and resulting high cost. This article reviews the research progress in TGV metallization to address the above issues. Several electroless plating studies were reported to improve the seed layer continuity and uniformity. Adjusting the concentrations of copper ions, chloride ions, accelerators, suppressors, and levelers in the plating bath are the keys to regulating the deposition rates at the via opening, via walls, and via center. Electroplating strategies, including pulsed electroplating and multistage electroplating, can optimize the electric-field distribution inside the vias. Ultrasonic agitation enhances forced convection within the vias and thereby improves mass-transport conditions. In summary, this paper reviews the research progress in TGV metallization, with emphasis on these key challenges and corresponding solutions, and discusses their prospects for high-density interconnects in advanced packaging.

## 關鍵內容 / Key Content

**TGV 金屬化的四個挑戰（作者分類）**
1. 高深寬比 TGV 內**種子層不連續**。
2. **銅離子濃度與電流密度分佈不均** ⇒ 孔內與孔口沉積速率差異顯著。
3. **空洞（voids）、縫（seams）、雙重橋接（double bridging）** —— 孔口沉積過快或中央橋接位置控制不良所致。
4. **填充率低 ⇒ 成本高**。

**對應解法（作者綜整）**
- **化學鍍（electroless plating）**：改善種子層連續性與均勻性。
- **鍍液配方**：銅離子、**氯離子**、加速劑（accelerator）、抑制劑（suppressor）、整平劑（leveler）濃度 —— 用以分別調控**孔口／孔壁／孔心**三處沉積速率。
- **電鍍策略**：**脈衝電鍍**、**多階段電鍍** ⇒ 優化孔內電場分佈。
- **超音波攪拌**：強化孔內強制對流，改善質傳。
