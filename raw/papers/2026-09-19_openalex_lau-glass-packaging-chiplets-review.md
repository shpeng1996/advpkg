# 摘要 / Abstract

In this study, glass packaging for chiplets heterogeneous integration has been presented. Emphasis has been placed on the applications of glass packaging to high-performance computing (HPC) and data centers driven by artificial intelligence (AI). Key topics such as organic-core substrate vs. glass-core substrate, glass-core substrate vs. glass-core interposer, glass-core interposer vs. organic-core interposer vs. silicon interposer, panel-level packaging vs. wafer-level packaging, status of panel size, effects of coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) of glass-core substrate on the solder joint reliability on printed circuit board, etc. have been discussed. Some recommendations have been provided.

4. 面板級封裝 vs 晶圓級封裝
