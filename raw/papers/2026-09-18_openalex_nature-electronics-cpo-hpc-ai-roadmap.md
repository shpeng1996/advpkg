# Co-packaged optics for high-performance computing and artificial intelligence

## 摘要 / Abstract（節錄原文）
"The large data movement required in high-performance computing and artificial intelligence workloads has exposed the fundamental limits of electrical interconnects, where **resistive losses, capacitive loading and frequency-dependent distortion** increasingly constrain bandwidth, latency and energy efficiency. Optical compute interconnects, which replace electrical links with co-packaged photonic channels, could provide low propagation loss, high bandwidth and superior signal integrity."

## 綜述結構 / Review scope
本文為 Nature Electronics 綜述，橫跨三層：
1. **電性子系統**
2. **電光／光電轉換介面**
3. **光傳輸網路**

並提出一份跨 **2D 共封裝光學 → 2.5D 中介層整合 → 3D 異質堆疊** 的技術路線圖。

## 關鍵論點 / Key claims
- 明確指出三項關鍵挑戰：**熱管理、可製造性、標準化**——與本 wiki 既有的 CPO 三大瓶頸敘述一致，但這是首次由頂級期刊綜述把三者並列為同級障礙。
- 路線圖以**封裝整合層級**（2D / 2.5D / 3D）而非以速率世代為軸，是與產業路線圖（TSMC COUPE 1.6→6.4→12.8 Tbps 等）不同的切法。
