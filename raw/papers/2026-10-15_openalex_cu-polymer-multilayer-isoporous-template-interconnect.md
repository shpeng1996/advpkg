# Precision Engineered 3D Multilayered Copper–Polymer Microstructures through Integrated Framework Fabrication

## 摘要 / Abstract（由 OpenAlex `abstract_inverted_index` 重建）

3D multilayered conductive microstructures are essential for advanced electronic materials, heterogeneous integration, and next-generation interconnect architectures. However, fabricating high-fidelity multilayered metallic composites remains challenging due to the limitations of existing methods, process complexity, and high cost. Here, we present a patented fabrication strategy based on advanced membrane nanotechnology for developing multilayered copper microstructures. The method uses engineered isoporous polymeric templates as structural scaffolds that guide the in situ and monolithic growth of copper across multiple layers. This approach enables control over copper-polymer layer composition and geometry, as demonstrated with multilayered architectures containing up to 10 distinct layers, with the potential for even more complex configurations. Structural and interfacial characterization confirms continuous copper growth, coherent interfaces, oxide-free copper regions, and through-thickness electrical communication across the multilayer architecture. Compared with conventional repeated layer-by-layer semiconductor fabrication routes, the proposed approach simplifies multilayer construction and shows potential to reduce electrochemical deposition time compared to traditional semiconductor methods. This methodology broadens the design space for developing customizable, rapidly manufactured microstructured materials for applications requiring high structural integrity.

## 關鍵內容 / Key content

- 方法：以**工程化等孔徑（isoporous）高分子模板**作結構骨架，引導銅**原位、整體式（in situ and monolithic）**跨多層成長，形成**銅—高分子多層微結構**。作者自述為**已申請專利之製程策略（patented fabrication strategy）**，基礎為「先進膜奈米技術（advanced membrane nanotechnology）」。
- 已驗證規模：**最多 10 個相異層**，並稱可更複雜。
- 表徵結論：**連續銅成長、共格界面（coherent interfaces）、無氧化物之銅區（oxide-free copper regions）**、以及**貫穿厚度方向的電性連通**。
- 宣稱優勢：相較傳統**逐層重複（layer-by-layer）**半導體製程，簡化多層構築，並**有潛力縮短電化學沉積（ECD）時間**。
