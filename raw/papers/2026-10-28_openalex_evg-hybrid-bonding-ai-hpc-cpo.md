# The Critical Role of Hybrid Bonding in driving Architectures for AI and High Performance Computing

## 摘要（OpenAlex 反向索引重建）

The relentless growth of AI workloads is pushing semiconductor packaging to its physical limits. To sustain performance gains, the industry must move beyond traditional packaging toward 3D stacking. This presentation highlights hybrid bonding as the key catalyst for this transition. By eliminating solder bumps and enabling ultra-fine pitch interconnects, hybrid bonding unlocks new possibilities for HBM integration and Co-Packaged Optics (CPO), drastically reducing the "memory wall" and I/O power consumption. These architectural shifts are essential for scaling AI accelerators and HPC systems to meet the computational demands of the next decade.

## 關鍵主張

- 混合接合為自傳統封裝轉向 3D 堆疊的**關鍵觸媒**
- 機制：**消除焊錫凸塊**＋**實現超細節距互連**
- 受益應用：**HBM 整合**與**共封裝光學（CPO）**
- 效益：大幅降低**記憶體牆**與 **I/O 功耗**
