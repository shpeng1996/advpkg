# TSMC Welcomes Intel's EMIB for More Market Flexibility; CEO Jokes About Memory Makers' Margin Boom

**2026-07-16 | TrendForce**

As market speculation grows that Intel and Samsung Electronics could benefit from TSMC's capacity constraints by capturing spillover demand, TSMC Chairman C.C. Wei addressed the trend at the Q2 2026 earnings call.

## Key Statements from C.C. Wei (Q2 2026 Earnings Call)

### On Intel EMIB and Market Flexibility
- TSMC's packaging capacity is **"so tight that it is now limiting customers' growth"**
- TSMC **welcomes "additional flexibility in the market"**
- Intel's EMIB technology **"looks good"** — hopes Intel can successfully scale up to help ease industry-wide capacity pressure
- More options in market = customers gain greater flexibility
- A more diversified advanced packaging landscape could **ultimately benefit TSMC** by supporting growth in its front-end wafer business

### Intel EMIB Gaining Traction (Context from Wccftech report, same date)
Intel's EMIB advanced packaging technology reportedly gaining traction among major AI chip players:
- **NVIDIA's Feynman GPU** — potential customer
- **Google's TPU HumuFish** — potential customer
- **Amazon's AWS Trainium 3** — potential customer

### On TSMC's Competitive Edge
Wei cautioned: "Choosing a technology and ramping it up is not like buying a bottle of milk from 7-Eleven" — no shortcuts in semiconductor industry; success comes down to fundamentals.

TSMC's three-pillar edge:
1. **Technology**
2. **Manufacturing excellence**
3. **Customer trust**

### On Memory Makers' Soaring Margins
- Wei jokingly said he was **"jealous"** of memory companies' **86% gross margins**
- TSMC views itself as long-term partner; does not want to squeeze customers
- TSMC does not intend to sharply raise prices by "4x or 5x"
- Philosophy: create value, maintain healthy gross margin, focus on long-term expansion — beneficial to both TSMC and customers

## Significance
This is the first time TSMC's CEO has publicly and positively characterized Intel EMIB as a welcome market development, representing a significant shift from competitive posturing to collaborative ecosystem framing. Combined with the $265B Arizona investment (same day announcement), TSMC appears to be positioning itself as the ultimate beneficiary of broader AI packaging ecosystem growth, even if Intel captures some advanced packaging orders.
