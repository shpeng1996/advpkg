Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan appointed Seok-Hee Lee as Executive Vice President of Intel Foundry (announced June 18, 2026). Lee joins Intel after serving as president and CEO of SK On and previously leading SK hynix. He also held engineering leadership roles at Intel and in academia.

Reporting directly to Tan, Lee will oversee advanced packaging, system integration, back-end technology development, and manufacturing. Intel said the appointment aims to strengthen system-level innovation capabilities and support the next phase of foundry expansion.

Tan underscored Lee's pivotal role: "As we prepare to scale advanced packaging technologies, including EMIB-T and HBI, for high-volume production, Seok-Hee is the ideal leader to drive the growth of this strategically important business."

The appointment comes after U.S. President Trump announced Apple had agreed to collaborate with Intel on designing and manufacturing chips in the United States. Google has tapped Intel to manufacture more than 3 million TPUs in 2028, and NVIDIA is evaluating Intel's 18A process and advanced packaging technologies for a next-generation multi-die GPU design.

Meanwhile, Intel Foundry EVP Naga Chandrasekaran continues reporting to CEO Lip-Bu Tan, overseeing front-end technology development and manufacturing (Intel 18A, Intel 14A, future nodes), design enablement, and customer-facing business operations.

## Intel's Talent Offensive Continues

This marks the latest in a series of high-profile hires since Lip-Bu Tan took the helm:
- April 2026: Samsung foundry veteran Shawn Han to support contract manufacturing
- Late 2025: Former TSMC SVP Wei-Ren Lo as EVP overseeing manufacturing and packaging
