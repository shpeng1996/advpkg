Intel Foundry's ECTC 2026 summary covering EMIB-T, co-packaged optics, and glass substrates.

## EMIB-T Highlights

- FLI (first layer interconnect) bump pitch scaled to **25 µm**.
- Package form factor up to **120 × 120 mm**.
- Integrates more than **9× reticles** of compute and memory Si on a single package.
- Signal performance: >12 Gb/s for HBM4e; **64 Gb/s UCIe interface**.
- EMIB-T enables disaggregated chiplet systems with near-monolithic performance.
- Collaboration with Siliconware Precision Industries (SPIL): 3D SRAM chiplet in fan-out embedded bridge.
- Research on "fluxless thermo-compression bonding of 4× reticle die stack."

## Co-Packaged Optics (CPO)

- **High-performance detachable edge optical connector**: glass fan-out couplers + expanded-beam edge connectors — low-loss, pluggable, compatible with high-volume manufacturing.
- **V-groove based glass coupler interface**: replaces fragile fiber-only FAU approach with a rigid optical glass coupler, improving manufacturability.

## Glass Core Substrates

- TGVs (through-glass vias): multiple types (low-taper, mixed sizes, cavities for embedded devices).
- Copper-filled TGVs survive harsh thermal cycling without failure.
- Glass stays flat and stable during processing vs. organic substrates that warp — enables precise multi-layer alignment.
- Supports 25 µm TGV pitch (demonstrated).
- Intel demonstrated scaling of first layer interconnect bump pitch to **25 µm with glass substrates**.

## Hybrid Bonding Research

- Collaboration with Fouriers Scientific: thermal behavior of hybrid bonding interfaces.
- Collaboration with NIST: copper plasticity via atomic force microscopy.
- Die distortion in D2W hybrid bonding.
- **Ultra-low temperature hybrid bonding** for D2W scaling.
- EV Group collaboration: advanced metrology for heterogeneous chiplet integration with **100% overlay control** at high speed.

## Other Research

- Large form factor Patch on Interposer (PoINT) scaling.
- Selective layer transfer for fine-grain mixed front-end devices.
- IR laser debond process modeling.
