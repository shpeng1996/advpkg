Global tech giants accelerating glass-based advanced packaging: Intel-Lens Technology TGV partnership, BOE full-scale AI semiconductor glass substrate entry, Samsung Display TGV interposer R&D team, Corning Glass Bridge TGV for CPO.

## Value Chain Structure

- **Upstream**: Specialty raw glass, ultrafast lasers, wet etching, PVD, electroplating
- **Midstream** (highest value): Glass thinning, TGV drilling, metallization/Cu filling, CMP, RDL → glass interposers or glass core substrates
- **Downstream**: AI advanced packaging — Chiplets, HBM, CPO; glass evolving into dual-function optoelectronic carriers

## 18 Listed Chinese Enterprises

### Midstream Leaders (end-to-end capability):

**Crystal-Optech**: In Corning optical communication ecosystem. Two vectors:
- HAMR glass platter substrates: pre-mass production small batches H2 2026
- Proprietary TGV optical interconnect substrates for 1.6T/3.2T CPO: joint R&D validation
- Exclusive domestic optical path supplier for Corning Glass Bridge; microlens samples H2 2026

**WG Tech** (subsidiary TGV Circuits): Self-developed ultrafast laser hybrid etching; minimum hole ≤5µm; aspect ratio 100:1. Wuhan facility: world's first glass-based TGV multilayer circuit board production line, planned 100K m²/year. Delivering small-batch 1.6T CPO carrier boards.

**BOE**: CNY 993M glass-based packaging substrate pilot line fully automated. TGV drilling and void-free deep-hole Cu filling mastered. 20-layer large-format glass carriers for AI chips delivered to leading AI vendors. May 2026: BOE-Corning 3-year MoU (Micro LED optical interconnects + glass-substrate CPO).

**TCL CSOT / Tianma Microelectronics**: Early R&D phase for TGV glass. TCL CSOT: technical exchanges with Corning.

## Equipment Vendors (Highest Near-Term Revenue)

**Hymson Laser**: Certified by leading North American compute giant; ultrafast laser + wet etching TGV end-to-end. Q1 2026 revenue CNY 1.317B (+144% YoY); returned to profitability.

**DR Laser**: TGV LACE (Laser-Assisted Chemical Etching) systems; panel-level TGV in mass shipments; overseas AP export orders in early 2026.

## Analyst Insight (TrendForce)

Panel makers have inherent glass knowledge advantage but face stringent reliability standards, heavy CapEx, and long qualification cycles. Display vs. semiconductor substrates differ fundamentally — cross-sector entry faces microcracking, thermal mismatch, signal degradation, and equipment incompatibility.

**Timeline**: TGV glass substrates likely enter mainstream advanced packaging **after 2030**. Currently in early validation; expected to complement rather than replace existing solutions.

Equipment suppliers offer highest earnings visibility and fastest order realization now.
