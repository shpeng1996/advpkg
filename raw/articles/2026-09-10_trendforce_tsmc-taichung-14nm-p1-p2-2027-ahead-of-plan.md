# TSMC's Taichung 1.4nm P1, P2 Fabs Could Begin Mass Production in 2027, Ahead of 2028 Plan

**Source:** TrendForce, citing Commercial Times, Economic Daily News (2026-09-10)

## TSMC Taichung 1.4nm Fab Status (Central Taiwan Science Park Phase 2)

Four 1.4nm (A14) fabs planned at Taichung CTSP Phase 2:

| Fab | Current Status | Completion | Mass Production Start |
|-----|---------------|------------|----------------------|
| P1 | Floor-slab construction | April 2027 | **2H27** (ahead of original 2028 plan) |
| P2 | Foundation construction | October 2027 | 2028 |
| P3 | Construction permit secured | Q2 2028 | 2028+ |
| P4 | Permit application underway | Q4 2028 | 2028+ |

- All 4 fabs expected completed by 2028
- Annual revenue estimate (once fully operational): ≥NT$500B (administration says "may be an underestimate")

## Key Schedule Acceleration

**P1 mass production could start 2H27**, previously planned for 2028 — significant pull-forward of ~6 months

## Supplier Cluster Formation

ASML, Tokyo Electron (TEL), and KLA have all established operations in **Taichung's 7th Redevelopment Zone** to support TSMC proximity.

## CPO/Advanced Packaging Link

TSMC rumored to be considering acquisition of **AUO's L7 and L5C facilities** in Central Taiwan Science Park, near Fab 15 — for **Co-Packaged Optics (CPO) large-format optoelectronic integration**.

## Competitive 1.4nm Race

| Company | 1.4nm Mass Production Target |
|---------|----------------------------|
| TSMC | **2H27** (P1, accelerated) |
| Intel (14A) | **2028** |
| Samsung | ~~2027~~ → **2029** (delayed) |

TSMC accelerating while Samsung slips by 2 years and Intel stays on track for 2028.
