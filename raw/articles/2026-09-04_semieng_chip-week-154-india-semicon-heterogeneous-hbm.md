# Chip Industry Week In Review #154

Published: September 4, 2026 | Semiconductor Engineering

## Key Topics This Week

**Semiconductor Policy & Investment:**
- **India Semicon 2.0**: India announced a $13.4 billion semiconductor program (Semicon 2.0), expanding its earlier $10B commitment with focus on advanced packaging, chip design, and fab capacity. This represents a significant escalation of India's effort to become a major semiconductor manufacturing hub.
- **$75M to recycle rare earths**: New US investment announced to build domestic rare earth recycling capacity for semiconductor materials.

**Advanced Packaging & Memory:**
- **Heterogeneous HBM**: Coverage of heterogeneous HBM integration approaches — combining different generation HBM stacks or logic dies within a single package.
- **SEMICON Taiwan 2026 highlights**: Recap of SEMICON Taiwan 2026 key announcements (TSMC equipment demand +90%, Samsung zHBM, Intel EMIB-T, CoPoS, CPO).
- **MediaTek deal**: Follow-up coverage on NVIDIA's $3.5B MediaTek ECB investment and implications for XPU ecosystem and advanced packaging supply chain (ASE, Sigurd).

**Silicon Photonics & Optical Interconnects:**
- **300mm Silicon Photonics**: MIT and NY CREATES researchers demonstrated a 300mm manufacturing process for flexible, transparent silicon photonics using standard semiconductor fabrication techniques — a significant step toward mainstream SiPh integration.
- **InP expansion**: Coverage of InP (indium phosphide) laser source expansion amid supply constraints driven by CPO demand.
- **PCIe 6/7 test**: New testing infrastructure for PCIe 6.0 and 7.0 interfaces critical for AI server interconnects.

**Research & Devices:**
- **2D Tunnel FET**: Hong Kong Polytechnic University researchers demonstrated a 2D tunnel transistor switching at lower voltages than conventional MOSFETs, room-temperature performance on cm-scale silicon substrates, meeting IRDS requirements for next-gen ICs.
- **In-memory photonic computing**: New research on photonic computing architectures that process data within the memory array.
- **Gain-Cell RAM**: Research into gain-cell DRAM alternatives for near-memory computing.

**AI & Automotive:**
- **Edge AI developer tools**: New tools for deploying AI at the edge, relevant to automotive and IoT applications.
- **Safe AI in SDVs**: Research and industry developments on AI safety frameworks for software-defined vehicles (SDVs).
- **GF PDKs**: GlobalFoundries released updated process design kits (PDKs) for designers targeting its advanced packaging and specialty process nodes.
