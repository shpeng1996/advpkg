# Chip Industry Week In Review #148

**Published**: July 24, 2026 | **Author**: The SE Staff | **Source**: Semiconductor Engineering

**Summary**: Amkor's $1.5B deal; Intel Foundry revenue up 31%; Siemens' dual buys; SiC plant dies; AMD's big week; TSMC price hike; Nokia's fab buy; earnings; $300M for accelerating inference; Keysight's multiphysics; copper interconnect alternative.

---

## Notable Deals

- **Amkor** and **Nvidia** signed a $1.5B multiyear agreement to develop advanced packaging and test technologies for next-gen AI and accelerated-computing platforms. Nvidia's prepayment will support Amkor's U.S. capacity expansion in Arizona, including high-density interconnect and heterogeneous integration capabilities.
- **Siemens** announced plans to acquire two EDA companies: **Defacto Technologies** (automated SoC design creation) and **Precision Innovations** (AI-driven chip planning based on OpenROAD framework).
- **IBM** plans to acquire **HRL Laboratories** (jointly owned by Boeing and GM) with expertise in silicon-spin qubits and quantum sensing.
- **Mitsubishi Electric** and **Sony Semiconductor** will establish a joint venture **Advanced Vision Solutions** for image sensors with integrated AI-based analysis.
- **Powertech Technology** is planning a **$400M joint venture with Broadcom** to provide panel-level packaging services in Singapore. (Source: Taipei Times, July 18, 2026)

## AMD's Full AI Stack

- **AMD** said its **Helios rack-scale AI platform has entered production**, combining 72 GPUs, 18 CPUs, and networking software for large-scale AI training and inference.
- **Anthropic** plans to deploy up to **2 GW** of AMD's GPUs in Helios racks, starting with 1GW in H1 2027. AMD will also invest up to $5B in Anthropic and use Claude for software development.
- **Cerebras** will combine AMD's rack-scale solution with its wafer-scale engine in a single inference workflow.
- AMD partners include **OpenAI, Meta, Microsoft**, and others.

## Price Hikes

- **TSMC** is reportedly planning to raise prices up to 10% in 2027, per Nikkei Asia.

## Facilities Watch

- **Nokia** is planning to acquire **NXP's Chandler, Arizona fab** to expand its indium phosphide (InP) semiconductor manufacturing capacity. Subject to approvals, Nokia plans to lease part of the site starting early next year, and convert it to InP production for optical components, with the full acquisition expected to close in Q1 2029.
- **SK Siltron** is closing its silicon carbide wafer plant in Monitor Township, Michigan.
- **Paras Semiconductors** signed an MoU for a ~US$644M OSAT facility in Madhya Pradesh, India.

## Big Funding

- **Etched** added $300M to scale production of its frontier model inference rack-scale system made up of co-designed chips, packages, PCBs, cold plates, and interconnects.
- **CuspAI** raised $450M for its AI materials discovery platform.

## Analysis / Market Data

- **Yole Group**: The advanced packaging market reached **$55B in 2025** and **could exceed $120B by 2031**. (Source: Yole Group press release)
- **TrendForce**: Expects NAND flash supply constraints to ease in H2 2027, driven by weakened demand for smartphones and notebooks.

## Earnings (Q2 2026)

- **Intel**: Posted its **fastest sales growth in 15 years**, with overall Q2 revenue **up 25% YoY**; **Intel Foundry revenue up 31% YoY**.
- **Besi**: Q2 2026 and H1 2026 results reported (see Besi investor relations).
- **Soitec**: Q1 FY2027 revenue reported.
- **STMicro**: Q2 2026 earnings reported.
- **Texas Instruments**: Q2 2026 financial results reported.

## Photonics

- Chinese optical transceiver maker **Zhongji InnoLight** planning +$7B Hong Kong IPO.
- **UIUC** team developed an integrated-THz tunable TFLN optical isolator.
- **Ghent U. and imec** demonstrated real-time optical-signal equalization using a silicon-photonic computing chip.

## Research / Technical Papers

- **Cornell**: Single-crystal niobium arsenide nanowires become more conductive as they shrink—potential **copper interconnect alternative** at advanced nodes.
- **Qualcomm**: "Known Good Reliable Die Screening in Chiplet-Based AI SoC" paper (arXiv).
- **Hanyang + imec**: TSV etch uniformity enhancement and aspect-ratio influence.
- **imec et al.**: 3D-DRAM architecture optimization with oxide-semiconductor channel (imec paper, IEEE Xplore).
- **UCSD**: In-storage retrieval accelerator that embeds vector-search functions directly into 3D NAND.
