# OpenAI Debuts Jalapeño AI Inference Chip, with Samsung Reportedly Supplying HBM4

Just ahead of NVIDIA's latest earnings report on August 26, OpenAI unveiled its first custom inference chip, Jalapeño. In initial benchmarks, it delivered 1.5×–1.9× higher throughput per kilowatt and 1.7×–3.6× lower end-to-end latency than NVIDIA's GB200 and GB300 rack systems. Notably, beyond the performance claims, Jalapeño could also emerge as a significant new contender for HBM4 supply.

## Key Specifications

- **Compute die**: TSMC 3nm process
- **Memory**: 6× HBM4 stacks → 216 GiB memory, 15.4 TB/s bandwidth
- **TDP**: 700W rated; sustained ≤550W during testing
- **Performance**: 1.5×–1.9× higher throughput/W vs NVIDIA GB200/GB300
- **Latency**: 1.7×–3.6× lower end-to-end latency vs NVIDIA GB200/GB300
- **ASIC partner**: Broadcom
- **HBM4 supplier**: Samsung (reportedly, per Economy Tribune and TokenPost)

## HBM4 Supply Implications

Each Jalapeño package combines its compute die with six HBM4 stacks, delivering 216 GiB of memory and 15.4 TB/s of bandwidth. By comparison, NVIDIA's GB300 features 288GB of HBM3e and carries a 1,400W power rating. On a rated-power basis, that gives Jalapeño roughly 50% more memory per watt.

Samsung Electronics is believed to supply the HBM4 for Jalapeño, with OpenAI handling architecture and Broadcom as its ASIC partner.

If OpenAI scales Jalapeño under its 10GW deployment deal with Broadcom (signed last October), the chip could become a major new claimant on HBM4 supply, adding pressure to a market where NVIDIA already holds substantial capacity.

## Roadmap

- Generation 1: TSMC 3nm, now deploying
- Generation 2: tape-out within months (Bloomberg)
- Generation 3: already in development

## Benchmark Caveats

- Not yet benchmarked against NVIDIA Vera Rubin (recently begun shipping)
- Not designed for AI training (inference-only)
- Tested on smaller open-source models including DeepSeek R1 and Kimi K2.5

## Advanced Packaging Significance

OpenAI Jalapeño uses TSMC 3nm + HBM4 packaging (6 stacks). This means OpenAI now competes in the same race for advanced wafers, HBM, and cutting-edge packaging capacity as NVIDIA's Blackwell and Rubin platforms. Samsung as HBM4 supplier (vs SK Hynix for NVIDIA) represents diversification of HBM4 customer base.
