China's push to strengthen its semiconductor equipment sector is accelerating. According to STAR Market Daily, the 14th China Semiconductor Equipment, Materials and Core Components Exhibition (CSEAC 2026) has opened, bringing together leading companies across the semiconductor supply chain. Notably, AMEC, China's leading etch equipment maker, held a product launch alongside the exhibition, unveiling several new high-end etching and thin-film deposition tools developed in-house.

## AMEC Launches New Etching and Deposition Tools at CSEAC 2026

For etching, AMEC introduced the **Primo XD-RIE™** system, designed for advanced 3D memory manufacturing with extremely high aspect ratios of 70:1 to 90:1. The tool uses very-high-frequency RF to optimize etch selectivity and a high-conductance architecture to broaden the process window, supporting the evolving requirements of advanced memory devices.

For thin-film deposition, AMEC unveiled four new tools targeting key processes in advanced 3D memory and logic chips:
- **Performo QuaStar® PECVD platform** (first product: Performo QuaStar® ON): targets silicon oxide/silicon nitride stack deposition in 3D NAND manufacturing. A single system can process up to 16 wafers simultaneously.
- **Prefima Unicore™ AM**: AMEC's self-developed 12-inch atomic layer deposition system for molybdenum, designed for word-line fill in ultra-high-stack 3D structures. Expected to become a key piece of equipment as advanced memory chips move toward higher layer counts.

The latest launches come just six months after AMEC unveiled four new products at SEMICON China in March, further expanding its high-end equipment portfolio across advanced logic, power semiconductors, thin-film deposition, and precision etching.

Other major participants at CSEAC 2026 included Naura, Piotech, and Leadmicro:
- **Piotech**: showcased deposition and 3D IC equipment, with PECVD tools ranking first in China in installed base and process coverage.
- **Leadmicro**: showcased thin-film solutions for **logic, advanced packaging, DRAM, and 3D NAND**, with CVD equipment having entered volume production.

## Wellrun Advances Domestic CD-SEM

Beyond etching and deposition, China's localization push is extending to semiconductor metrology and inspection. **Wellrun Microelectronics** has developed 12-inch CD-SEM (Critical Dimension Scanning Electron Microscope) systems for 22nm volume production and 14nm R&D. CD-SEM has the second-lowest localization rate among semiconductor equipment categories in China, behind only lithography tools.

Several of Wellrun's metrology and inspection tools have reportedly been deployed and are running steadily on mature-node production lines in China. At CSEAC, the company showcased its 14nm and 22nm CD-SEM systems, photomask critical-dimension metrology equipment, and an AI-based e-beam metrology platform. Wellrun has also signed a strategic partnership with major Chinese IDM CR Micro to advance domestic substitution of semiconductor metrology and inspection equipment.
