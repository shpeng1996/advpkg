# Chinese AI Chipmakers Reportedly Hike Prices on HBM Costs, Huawei 950DT Up as Much as 50%

**Source:** TrendForce, citing Reuters, Seoul Economic Daily, The Information, Bloomberg (2026-09-10)

Rising memory costs are adding pricing pressure across China's AI chip market, with major domestic suppliers reportedly raising prices for their latest products.

## Price Hike Details

- **Huawei Ascend 950DT**: Price raised to >RMB 250,000 per card (+20-50% from two months ago). 4Q26 availability announced.
- **Cambricon 690** (next-gen, not yet formally launched): +20-30% above levels indicated two months ago
- **MetaX** and **Iluvatar CoreX**: Similar price increases
- **Huawei Ascend 950PR**: RMB 60,000 (start of 2026) → >RMB 80,000 (~+30%)
- **Huawei Ascend 910C**: ~RMB 90,000 → >RMB 110,000

## HBM Shortage Root Cause

Since Washington tightened export controls on advanced HBM to China in **December 2024**, Chinese chipmakers increasingly source HBM via grey-market channels at **several times** the market price outside China. Memory accounts for a significant share of AI accelerator production cost.

**Huawei proprietary HBM technologies** (undisclosed manufacturing source):
- **HiBL 1.0** — for Ascend 950PR
- **HiZQ 2.0** — for Ascend 950DT

## CXMT HBM3E Development

According to Seoul Economic Daily (citing The Information):
- **CXMT** has begun **small-scale production of HBM3E**
- Plans to **significantly expand output in 2027**
- Chinese chip designers including **Cambricon** and **Alibaba T-Head** are testing CXMT HBM3E

## Supply Allocation Dynamics

- **Iluvatar CoreX**: Doubled GPU shipments to ByteDance to **100,000 units** this year; reassigned GPUs from internal use
- **ByteDance AI chip supplier ranking**: Huawei #1 → Cambricon #2 → Iluvatar CoreX #3
- **DeepSeek**: Plans to deploy **≥160,000 Ascend 950DT chips** at new Inner Mongolia data center (Bloomberg/TechNews)

## Strategic Implications

- China's domestic HBM supply chain is accelerating (CXMT HBM3E trial production is the first confirmed milestone)
- Grey-market HBM cost premium directly driving accelerator card price increases
- Price increases could slow China AI infrastructure build-out, or accelerate CXMT HBM domestication pressure
