# Huawei Unveils 7.2Tbps Near-packaged Optics Module, Challenging NVIDIA and Broadcom's CPO Push

**Source:** TrendForce, 2026-09-11
**Event:** China International Optoelectronic Exposition (CIOE)

## Summary

As NVIDIA, Broadcom and TSMC rally behind co-packaged optics (CPO), Huawei is charting a different course with near-packaged optics (NPO). Huawei unveiled its own NPO standard and debuted a 7.2Tbps solution at CIOE.

## Key Technical Specifications

- **7.2Tbps NPO module**: 36 channels × 200Gbps each
- Comparison: Broadcom CPO optical engines = 6.4Tbps
- **Deployment timeline**: 3.2Tbps NPO in real-world deployment this year (2026); 7.2Tbps broader adoption ~2 years

## NPO vs. CPO Architecture

- **CPO** (Co-packaged Optics): integrates optical components alongside electronics at chip-package level using 2.5D/3D advanced packaging; optical engines (photonic chips) directly on same substrate as switch ASICs or compute chips. Lower latency, lower signal loss, greater bandwidth density.
- **NPO** (Near-packaged Optics): intermediate between pluggable modules and CPO; integrates at **PCB level** rather than inside chip package; **pluggable architecture** allows faulty component replacement.

## OPEN NPO Initiative

Huawei joined China Mobile Research Institute, JD Cloud and 20+ supply-chain partners to launch "OPEN NPO" — China's first NPO optical interconnect Multi-Source Agreement (MSA):
- First technical specification: Q3 2026
- Large-scale NPO commercialization target: 1H 2027
- Barriers: fragmented interface standards, limited interoperability, weak supply-chain coordination

## Market Context

- **CPO/NPO combined market**: ~$100M (2025) → $39B+ (2030) — TrendForce projection
- Chinese CSPs (Alibaba, Tencent) favor NPO as core medium-term strategy; promoting via ODCC (Open Data Center Committee)
- Meta and Microsoft also pursuing NPO via OCI-MSA (Open Compute Interconnect MSA)
- CPO supporters: NVIDIA, Broadcom, TSMC, Intel, Marvell — CPO solutions entered production 2H 2026

## Competitive Positioning

Huawei's NPO approach enables China to develop optics interconnects without depending on the advanced packaging integration stack (2.5D/3D) that is currently dominated by TSMC and western technology. This is a strategic alternative track.
