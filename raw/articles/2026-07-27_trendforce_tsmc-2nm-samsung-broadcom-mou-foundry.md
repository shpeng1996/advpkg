# [News] TSMC Scales 2nm Capacity as Samsung Wins Major AI Chip Deal with Broadcom

2026-07-27 | TrendForce (citing Wccftech, SEDaily, Maeil Business, Economic Daily News)

## Samsung-Broadcom MOU

Samsung has signed an MOU with Broadcom to collaborate across memory and foundry technologies. The Samsung-Broadcom partnership will focus on the ASIC market, with Broadcom planning to use Samsung's HBM4 memory in its AI accelerators. Samsung is also expected to ramp production of its next-generation HBM4E memory to strengthen the collaboration.

By combining its 2nm process with 1c DRAM technology, Samsung can co-optimize chip design, power delivery, and thermal performance—a vertically integrated approach that could give it an advantage over TSMC.

TSMC's planned foundry price increases have created an opportunity for Samsung. TSMC reportedly informed major customers that it plans to raise foundry prices by up to 10% starting in 2027. The price of its 2nm process, currently about US$30,000 per wafer, is expected to exceed US$33,000 after the hike.

Samsung has also secured production orders for Tesla's next-generation AI6 chips and recently signed an MOU with Anthropic covering the design, manufacturing, and packaging of 2nm-based AI chips.

Industry observers say Samsung's success will ultimately depend on customer qualification, 2nm yields, advanced packaging mass-production capabilities, and final customer orders.

## TSMC Accelerates 2nm Capacity Expansion

TSMC is also accelerating its 2nm capacity expansion. Demand for the company's 2nm process remains strong, with mass production progressing rapidly across five fabs in Taiwan. The Baoshan site reached a monthly capacity of 20,000 wafers in May 2026 and continues to ramp. Together with new capacity coming online in Kaohsiung, production capacity for TSMC's 2nm family is expected to expand month by month.

The 2nm family will expand to include N2P, with mass production scheduled for the second half of 2026.

Key metrics:
- TSMC plans to ramp production at five 2nm fabs
- First-year 2nm wafer output target: +45% compared with first year of 3nm (2023)
- CAGR target for 2nm capacity 2026–2028: 70%
- 2nm accounted for 3% of TSMC Q2 wafer revenue; 3nm=30%, 5nm=33%, 7nm=11%
- Advanced processes (7nm and below): 77% of total wafer revenue in Q2 2026
- TSMC 2nm wafer price currently ~$30,000; projected >$33,000 after 2027 hike
