# Intel raises $19.7 billion to help fund future projects as 14A production looms

**Source:** Tom's Hardware | **Author:** Anton Shilov | **Published:** 2026-08-11

Intel is set to raise $19.7 billion by selling new common stock in a bid to finance the building out of new production capacity, the development of next-generation leading-edge process technologies like 14A, and day-to-day operations.

## Key Facts

### The Stock Offering
- Intel sold 210,526,315 shares at **$95 per share** through an underwritten public offering
- Underwriting banks have 30 days to acquire up to 31,578,947 more shares at $95
- If all options exercised: ~242.1 million shares total, ~$23 billion proceeds
- **Net proceeds: approximately $19.7 billion** (after discounts, commissions, and expenses)
- Transaction closed: **August 12, 2026**
- The share sale attracted **$100 billion in demand** (Bloomberg report), signaling strong investor confidence

### Intel's Financial Context
- Market capitalization increased from ~$90 billion (August 2025) to **$491 billion at press time**
- Intel all-time high market cap: **$673 billion on June 20, 2026**
- Company needs to compete vs. TSMC and Samsung which spend tens of billions annually on fabs

### Use of Proceeds
Intel has not assigned the money to particular projects; proceeds can be used for:
- Capital expenditures and working capital across the business
- **Fab 52 (Arizona)** ramping; adjacent **Fab 62** ready for when needed
- **Ohio fab complex** (expected to cost >$100 billion when fully built)
- **Intel 14A** — due to enter mass production in 2028
- Other advanced process technologies and manufacturing expansion
- Securing design wins and volume commitments from external foundry customers

### Intel 14A and Foundry Status
- Intel 14A: risk production in 2H 2027; **HVM 2028** (confirmed Q2 2026 earnings)
- Fab 52 (Arizona): currently ramping with 18A
- **Tesla** confirmed as first 14A external customer
- Intel also mentioned alternative financing arrangements, government grants, and **U.S. government equity position** as factors

### Advanced Packaging Context
The capital raise directly funds Intel Foundry's expansion, which includes:
- EMIB-T scale-up (HVM 2027 target)
- HBI (Hybrid Bonding Interconnect) ramp
- Additional packaging-focused production capacity in U.S. and Vietnam
- Intel had previously completed $20B capital raise (upsized from $15B initial announcement)

## Market Significance
This is one of the largest equity capital raises in semiconductor history. The $100 billion demand vs. $20 billion supply (5× oversubscription) reflects investor conviction in Intel's foundry turnaround. Combined with:
- Seok-Hee Lee (SK Hynix ex-CEO) joining as Foundry EVP (June 2026)
- Tesla as first 14A customer
- NVIDIA/Google/Amazon evaluating EMIB

The raise signals Intel Foundry is transitioning from skepticism to institutional investability.
