# Nvidia certifies 'big three' for HBM4, securing memory supply for Vera Rubin launch: report

By Ben Wodecki, SDxCentral, 2026-06-05

## Key Claims

1. **All three major memory makers (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron) certified** by NVIDIA to supply HBM4 for the Vera Rubin platform — Jensen Huang confirmed at Computex 2026: "All three vendors are in production, and they're all racing to support Vera Rubin"
2. **Samsung** began HBM4 mass production in February 2026; received NVIDIA qualification nod in January 2026; required threshold: ≥10 Gbps data transfer speed
3. **SK Hynix** had previously pushed back some HBM4 production timelines (reports of potential Rubin delay); now back on track and certified
4. **Micron** certified alongside the other two — smaller share but now formally in the Vera Rubin supply chain
5. HBM supply lead times pushed to **as far as 2028** amid ongoing memory shortage

## Key Data Points

| Supplier | HBM4 Status (June 2026) | Est. Share |
|----------|------------------------|------------|
| SK Hynix | Certified + producing; ~70% vol | ~60–70% |
| Samsung | Certified since Jan; HBM4 mass prod since Feb | ~25–30% |
| Micron | Certified; smaller volume | ~5–10% |

- Vera Rubin entered full production at Computex 2026 (June 1–5, 2026), Taipei
- Jensen Huang: "All three vendors are in production"
- Memory shortage impact: some prior reports suggested Rubin shipments might drop from 29% to 22% of forecast due to memory supply

## Supply Chain Context

- NVIDIA invested in optical supply chain (Ayar Labs, Lumentum, Corning, Coherent) alongside memory suppliers
- Jensen Huang approach: prepayments, long-term forecasts, equity investments in critical supply chain nodes
- TSMC CEO C.C. Wei also warned AI demand could outpace chip supply for "years"
- Memory shortage: HBM lead times extended to 2028 for some customers

## New Knowledge Added

- Confirms **first HBM4 triple-supplier qualification event** in industry history — previously Samsung + SK Hynix were the only established HBM suppliers; Micron's certification marks its entry as a third HBM4 vendor for NVIDIA flagship products
- Provides the supply-side context for why Samsung HBM4 yield improvement to ~80% (Aug 2026) matters: Samsung is actively competing with SK Hynix for Vera Rubin share

## Contradictions / Corrections

- Wiki notes Samsung was not in NVIDIA's top-5 customers in 1H26 (TrendForce, Aug 2026) — this article (June 2026) explains the supply dynamic: Samsung was certified but with lower allocation (~25–30%) vs SK Hynix dominant share
