# Samsung, CXMT Reportedly Supply LPDDR5X for Chinese AI Phone as Domestic Memory Enters Flagship Supply Chain

**Source:** TrendForce, 2026-09-11 (citing Business Korea)
**Event:** Nubia NaviX Ultra (ZTE + ByteDance) smartphone launch

## Summary

China's CXMT is making further inroads into the high-end mobile memory market. Samsung Electronics and CXMT are jointly supplying LPDDR5X for the Nubia NaviX Ultra (jointly developed by ZTE and ByteDance), establishing a multi-vendor memory supply system in a Chinese flagship smartphone.

## CXMT LPDDR5X Technical Progress

- CXMT started LPDDR5X mass production May 2025 at 8.533–9.6Gbps (behind Samsung/SK hynix)
- By September 2026: CXMT LPDDR5X max speed = **10.667Gbps** — now essentially equivalent to Samsung/SK hynix top-tier
- Competitiveness gaps remain: power efficiency, heat generation, yield

## CXMT LPDDR6 Milestone (September 2026)

- CXMT LPDDR6 entered mass production (already in wiki from 2026-09-08 article)
- Used in **Xiaomi 18 Fold** (world's first commercial LPDDR6 flagship phone)
- 12,800Mbps; 60% higher bandwidth and 20% lower power vs. LPDDR5X

## Geopolitical Significance

- High-end Chinese smartphones historically relied on Korean memory (Samsung/SK hynix)
- CXMT's entry into flagship supply chain marks strategic shift toward domestic memory independence
- Multi-vendor supply system (Korean + Chinese memory) in top-tier Chinese smartphones — first of its kind
