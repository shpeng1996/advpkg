# Intel Pulls Forward 14A Timeline by One Year, Targeting 2028 Mass Production Near TSMC's Roadmap

**Source:** TrendForce (citing Reuters, Wccftech, CNBC, Yahoo Finance, Intel) | **Date:** 2026-07-24 | **URL:** https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/07/24/news-intel-pulls-forward-14a-timeline-by-one-year-targeting-2028-mass-production-near-tsmcs-roadmap/

## Intel Q2 2026 Earnings Beat

Intel delivered stronger-than-expected Q2 2026 results: fastest quarterly revenue growth since 2011. Intel hiked its 2026 CapEx target to more than US$20 billion from US$18 billion. Adjusted EPS of 42 cents on revenue of US$16.1 billion, beating LSEG estimates of 21 cents and US$14.42 billion. Intel Foundry generated US$5.77 billion in quarterly revenue, exceeding analysts' estimate of US$5.55 billion.

Q3 2026 guidance: Revenue of US$15.8 billion to US$16.8 billion (above analysts' forecast of US$15.1 billion).

## 14A Timeline Pulled Forward

- **Risk production (internal products)**: 2H 2027 (one year earlier than previously planned)
- **High-volume manufacturing (HVM)**: 2028 (ahead of original 2029 roadmap; matches TSMC timeline)
- 14A PDK 0.5: complete; PDK 0.9: on track for October 2026
- 14A is progressing ahead of 18A in both defect density and transistor performance (CEO Lip-Bu Tan)
- SRAM 256 yield and defect density ahead of schedule
- First 14A customer confirmed: **Tesla** (via TeraFab, Austin, AI complex; confirmed by Elon Musk in April)
- "Increasing momentum on customer engagements for Intel 14A" — CEO Lip-Bu Tan

## 18A Progress

- Intel 18A entered mass production in late 2025
- Panther Lake (Core Ultra Series 3) and Wildcat Lake (Core Series 3) upcoming products on 18A
- Intel Foundry reduced cost of primary Panther Lake SKU by ~50% YTD; expects further 20% reduction; meaningful savings in 2027
- Panther Lake margins expected to rise above company average as yield improves
- 18A achieved strongest ramp among Intel 7, Intel 3, and 18A in Q2; 18A-P risk production started
