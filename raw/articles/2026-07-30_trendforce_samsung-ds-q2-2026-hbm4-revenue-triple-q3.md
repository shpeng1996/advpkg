Samsung Electronics delivered another record second quarter, with operating profit jumping 19-fold year over year to KRW 89.5 trillion. The DS (Device Solutions) division accounted for 99.7% of Samsung's total operating profit in Q2, with operating profit soaring more than 250-fold YoY.

Samsung posted record Q2 revenue of KRW 171.5 trillion, up 28% QoQ. The DS unit generated KRW 89.2 trillion in operating profit, with its operating margin expanding to nearly 70% from 65.7% in Q1 2026.

However, as soaring memory prices drove up component costs, Samsung's Mobile eXperience (MX) division posted a KRW 700 billion operating loss—its first quarterly loss on record.

## Memory Shortage to Worsen in 2027

Samsung expects the memory supply crunch to intensify in H2, as surging demand for AI server DRAM, enterprise SSDs, and HBM far outpaces weakening mobile and PC demand. Samsung also forecast that the supply shortage in AI memory will worsen in 2027 and the imbalance is expected to persist through 2028.

## HBM4 Progress

- Samsung already in HBM4 mass production; shipping to major customers including NVIDIA
- HBM4E sample shipments to key clients underway
- Samsung expects HBM4 revenue to more than TRIPLE QoQ in Q3 2026
- HBM4 to account for more than 60% of total HBM revenue in H2 2026
- Plans to expand HBM4 supply capacity alongside 1c-class DRAM production ramp
- Samsung aims to secure HBM market share comparable to its overall DRAM share

## ASP and Shipment Data (Q2 2026)

- Samsung DRAM ASPs: +mid-40% QoQ
- Samsung NAND ASPs: +high-60% QoQ
- DRAM shipments Q2: +10%+ QoQ
- NAND shipments Q2: +low-single-digit QoQ
- (vs SK hynix: DRAM ASP +30% QoQ; NAND ASP +mid-50% QoQ)

## System LSI and Foundry

Samsung's Foundry and System LSI units remained loss-making in Q2, but signs of improvement emerged:
- Foundry earnings improved on rising HBM base-die demand and U.S. customer orders
- New 2nm HPC design wins expanding
- H2 2026: Foundry aims to scale 2nm mobile chip production and accelerate 4nm LPU and base-die products
