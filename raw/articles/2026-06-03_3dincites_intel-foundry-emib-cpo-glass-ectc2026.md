# How Intel Foundry Packaging Technologies Redefine AI and HPC Scalability Limits

**Published**: June 3, 2026 | **Author**: Lori Scott, Senior Director of Marketing, Intel Foundry | **Source**: IMAPS 3D InCites Content Platform

---

## Overview

Intel Foundry highlighted three major advanced packaging technologies at ECTC 2026: **EMIB-T**, **Co-Packaged Optics (CPO)**, and **Glass Core Substrates**.

---

## 1. EMIB-T Scaling

EMIB-T enables ultra-large, high-performance chiplet systems that scale beyond silicon reticle limits and traditional packaging constraints.

**Key specifications demonstrated at ECTC 2026**:
- FLI bump pitch scaled to **25 µm**
- Package form factor: up to **120 × 120 mm**
- Integrates more than **9× reticles** of compute and memory Si content on a single package
- **12 Gb/s for HBM4e** interface performance
- **64 Gb/s UCIe** interface performance
- Power delivery: TSV-enabled vertical scaling with **MIM capacitors + Cu ground layer**

**Collaborations**:
- Intel + **SPIL (Siliconware Precision Industries)**: 3D integration of SRAM chiplet in fan-out embedded bridge
- Package architectures for hyper large form-factors for AI and HPC
- Research on challenges and solutions for package-level ultra-large die complexes

---

## 2. Co-Packaged Optics (CPO)

CPO moves optical-to-electrical signal conversion directly to the package, enabling higher bandwidth, lower power, and more scalable systems.

### V-Groove Glass Coupler (Key Innovation)

- Traditional CPO edge coupling uses fiber array units (FAUs) inserted into V-grooves etched into silicon — a difficult and fragile process
- Intel Foundry replaces this with a **rigid optical glass coupler** that attaches to the V-grooves in photonic integrated circuits (PICs)
- Benefits: simplifies manufacturing, improves bandwidth and energy efficiency, reduces latency
- Edge-coupled optical interface: **glass fan-out couplers + expanded-beam edge connectors**
- Delivers low-loss, highly reliable, and **detachable** optical interfaces compatible with high-volume manufacturing
- Enables **serviceable** AI scale-up networks in compact systems

Other CPO research: die-to-die attach with low thermal input thermo-compression bonding

---

## 3. Glass Core Substrates

### Why Glass Substrates?

Traditional organic core substrates are reaching their limit for scaling. Problems:
- Organic substrates **warp and shift** during processing
- Poor precision for layer alignment in multi-chiplet systems

Glass advantages:
- **Stays flat and stable**, allowing extremely precise alignment
- Higher performance heterogeneous integration
- Better power delivery and stability
- Low warpage for large AI and HPC packages
- Electrical and optical integration on one platform

### Through-Glass Via (TGV) Technology

Intel Foundry capabilities:
- **Low taper TGVs** 
- **Mixed TGV sizes**
- **Cavities for embedding devices**
- **Fully copper-filled TGVs** survive very harsh thermal cycling **without failure** (confirmed long-term reliability)
- Enables flexible package designs
