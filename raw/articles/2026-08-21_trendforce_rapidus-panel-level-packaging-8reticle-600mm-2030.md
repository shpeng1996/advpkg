# Rapidus Scales Up Advanced Packaging, Targets 8-Reticle Interposers With 600mm Panels by 2030

**Published:** 2026-08-21 | **Source:** TrendForce (citing TechNews, ICVIEWS, EE Times, Rapidus, EE Times Japan, Nikkei)

Advanced packaging is taking on a bigger role as AI reshapes how semiconductor systems are designed and manufactured. Rapidus is responding with a roadmap centered on larger interposers and panel-level packaging. According to TechNews, **Rozalia Beica**, CTO of packaging at Rapidus Design Solutions, outlined the company's strategy at the **2026 OCP APAC Summit**, highlighting a broader industry shift from chip scaling toward system-level integration.

## 8-Reticle Interposer Roadmap

Rapidus is targeting **eight-reticle-size interposers** for high-performance chips by around 2030. Beica outlined a roadmap:
- 4 reticles → 3,320 mm²
- 6 reticles → 4,980 mm²
- 8 reticles → 6,640 mm²

By 2030, the accompanying **organic substrate** is expected to reach **14,400 mm²**, setting the package-size target for Rapidus' 600 × 600 mm panel-level advanced processor packaging.

For comparison, TSMC is moving even faster on interposer scaling:
- 2026: 5.5-reticle CoWoS
- 2027: 9.5-reticle
- 2028: 14-reticle

However, the two companies are pursuing different manufacturing approaches — Rapidus bets on **600 × 600 mm panel-level processing** for large-format interposers.

## Panel Output Comparison

Beica compared output of an eight-reticle-size interposer across four formats:
- 4 units on a 300 mm wafer
- 9 units on a 300 × 300 mm panel
- 36 units on a 510 × 515 mm panel
- **49 units on a 600 × 600 mm panel**

## 600mm Panel-Level Packaging

Rapidus has already laid the groundwork for its 600mm packaging strategy. Under its FY2026 plan, the company is bringing its **Rapidus Chiplet Solutions (RCS) pilot line** into full-scale operation to verify 2.xD and 3D packaging processes, while advancing 600 × 600 mm panel-level packaging for high-density chiplet integration.

Rapidus is tapping **Lam Research's Kallisto electroplating system** for 2.xD packaging with RDL (redistribution layer) formed on 600mm-square glass carriers. Rapidus unveiled what it claims is the world's first large-format glass interposer prototype in late 2025, with mass production targeted for 2028.

## IIM Model: Front-End and Back-End Integration

Beyond panel-level packaging, Rapidus is developing a new manufacturing model Beica calls **"Innovative Integration and Manufacturing" (IIM)** — effectively the world's first fab to integrate front-end and back-end processes under one roof. The model uses AI and sensor data to monitor wafer conditions in real time, predict defects, and feed insights back into design, enabling joint optimization of design and manufacturing.

Rapidus' advanced packaging portfolio will include:
- Flip-chip BGA
- 2.5D silicon interposers
- Panel-level RDL organic interposers
- Solutions with or without bridges
- 3D stacking with hybrid bonding

## 2030 Vision

Looking toward 2030, Beica expects future systems to integrate: sub-2nm logic + high-bandwidth memory + chiplets + optical interconnects + hybrid bonding + panel-level packaging.
