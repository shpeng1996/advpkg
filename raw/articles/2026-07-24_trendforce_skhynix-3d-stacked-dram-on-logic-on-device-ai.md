# SK hynix Reportedly Hires for 3D-Stacked DRAM-on-Logic With a U.S. Customer; Targets On-Device AI

**Source:** TrendForce (citing ZDNet) | **Date:** 2026-07-24 | **URL:** https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/07/24/news-sk-hynix-reportedly-hires-for-3d-stacked-dram-on-logic-with-a-u-s-customer-targets-on-device-ai/

SK hynix is reportedly stepping up development of 3D-stacked DRAM, a next-generation memory technology for on-device AI. The company has reportedly begun recruiting 3D-Stacked DRAM-on-Logic Design Engineers through its U.S. subsidiary in San Jose, California. SK hynix is also said to have partnered with a specific customer to develop applications for the technology. This marks the first time SK hynix has recruited specialists dedicated to 3D-stacked DRAM.

## 3D-Stacked DRAM-on-Logic: Architecture Overview

In the job posting, SK hynix said it is seeking engineers to work closely with a U.S. customer to co-design logic dies for 3D-Stacked DRAM-on-Logic, an advanced packaging architecture that vertically integrates DRAM directly on top of a system-on-chip. Unlike package-on-package (PoP), which stacks separately packaged chips, 3D-Stacked DRAM-on-Logic enables much shorter interconnects and more data I/O channels within the same footprint. This can reduce data-transfer latency while improving power efficiency and space utilization.

The technology is being developed primarily for on-device AI. Because these applications require both high memory bandwidth and low power consumption, conventional package-on-package (PoP) architectures have been identified as a performance bottleneck. Mobile application processors (APs) are viewed as one of the technology's most promising applications. Apple and Qualcomm are among the leading designers of mobile APs.

## Alignment with SK hynix's Roadmap (TSMC Symposium 2026)

SK hynix President and Chief Development Officer (CDO) Hyun Ahn said AI development is increasingly constrained by memory bottlenecks, requiring new memory architectures beyond incremental improvements. He said the company is working with TSMC to apply its advanced logic process to HBM4 base dies, with plans to expand the technology to High Bandwidth Flash (HBF) and 3D-Stacked DRAM-on-Logic to further integrate memory and logic for higher performance and power efficiency.

## Samsung's Parallel Research

Samsung Electronics is also advancing research into next-generation 3D DRAM. The company recently published a joint paper with imec, KU Leuven, and Lam Research on a monolithic 3D DRAM architecture using oxide semiconductor channels. The research remains at the simulation stage, with no commercialization plans disclosed.

## Key Data Points

- SK hynix: first-ever 3D-Stacked DRAM-on-Logic dedicated recruitment (San Jose, U.S. subsidiary)
- Architecture: DRAM directly integrated on SoC via 3D vertical stacking (shorter I/O, lower latency vs. PoP)
- Target application: on-device AI (mobile AP, Apple/Qualcomm are leading mobile AP designers)
- SK hynix roadmap sequence: HBM4 base die (TSMC 12nm) → HBF → 3D-Stacked DRAM-on-Logic
- Samsung: joint paper (imec + KU Leuven + Lam Research) on monolithic 3D DRAM (oxide semiconductor channels); simulation stage only
