With memory champions CXMT and YMTC tapping capital markets, China's National Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund (the "Big Fund") Phase III, launched in 2024, marks a notable shift from its earlier phases, with investments increasingly focused on advanced packaging, semiconductor equipment and materials, and computing chips.

## Phase I & II: Building China's Semiconductor Foundation

Big Fund Phase I (2014, ~RMB 130B) invested in 23 companies across 75 projects: SMIC, Huahong, JCET, Tongfu, NAURA, Empyrean EDA, YMTC.

Phase II (2019, ~RMB 200B) emphasized supply chain amid US restrictions, prioritizing wafer fabrication. Largest single investment: US$1.5B into SMIC. Phase II holds ~8.73% of CXMT; Phases I+II hold 11.97%+11.38% of YMTC.

## Phase III: Three Strategic Pillars

Phase III has organized around three pillars:

1. **Advanced Packaging and IP Integration** (led by Huaxin Dingxin):
   - Tiansui Xinyuan Technology (31.58% stake, RMB 300M): automotive-grade vision ISP and display processing IP from Zhudian Semiconductor / VeriSilicon
   - Anhui Juhe Microelectronics (18% stake): Chiplet packaging substrates and heterogeneous integration; one of few independent packaging platforms in China for high-volume Chiplet production

2. **Equipment and Materials** (led by Guotou Jixin — most active):
   - Flagship: RMB 450M in Tuojing Jianke (Piotech spin-off): HBM hybrid bonding and chip-stacking equipment

3. **AI Computing Chips**:
   - YunSilicon: high-speed interconnect and compute-network switching
   - TsingMicro: compute-in-memory AI chips and on-device LLM processors
   - Beijing Aijieko Xin: general-purpose AI accelerators and generative AI chips

## Significance

Phase III signals China's shift from capacity-building to leapfrog strategies via 3D ICs, heterogeneous integration, and AI chip design — targeting critical chokepoints rather than broad fab expansion.
