# CXMT Starts LPDDR6 Mass Production at 12,800Mbps; Open to Global Customers as Apple Ties Draw Attention

**Published:** September 8, 2026 | **Source:** TrendForce

## Key Announcement

China's leading DRAM maker CXMT has officially announced mass production of LPDDR6, with the next-generation memory making its commercial debut in Xiaomi's newly launched **Xiaomi 18 Fold** flagship foldable.

**CXMT LPDDR6 specs:**
- Peak data rate: **12,800 Mbps**
- Operating speed with SoC: **10,667 Mbps**
- Die capacity: **16Gb** dies
- Package: **1,295-ball FBGA**, compliant with JEDEC LPDDR6 standard
- Performance vs LPDDR5X (10,667 Mbps): **+60% peak bandwidth, -20% power consumption**

## CXMT Market Position

Per TrendForce, CXMT's global DRAM revenue share:
- **2Q26**: 9.5% (up from 7.6% in 1Q26) — **fourth globally**
- Samsung: 39.4% | SK hynix: 24.9% | Micron: 23.3% | CXMT: 9.5%

GM Zhao Lun stated: CXMT products are now competitive with leading international suppliers in performance, quality, and supply stability.

## Competitive LPDDR6 Landscape

| Company | LPDDR6 Speed | Process | Status |
|---------|-------------|---------|--------|
| CXMT    | 12,800 Mbps | Not disclosed | Mass production (Sept 2026, Xiaomi 18 Fold) |
| SK hynix | 14,400 Mbps (target) | 1c (10nm-class) | Development complete Mar 2026; HVM 2H 2026 |
| Samsung  | 12,800 Mbps | 12nm | Showcased CES 2026; no HVM timeline |

## Apple Ties and HBM Ambitions

**Apple:** GM Zhao Lun said CXMT remains open to exploring partnerships with "high-quality customers worldwide." CXMT's capacity is **fully booked through 2027 at 95% utilization** — limiting full absorption of Apple's ~600 million GB demand (50M units × 12GB).

**HBM development:** CXMT allocated **RMB 9 billion** of its RMB 29.5 billion IPO proceeds specifically for HBM and related **advanced packaging technologies** as key development priorities.

## Advanced Packaging Relevance

CXMT's HBM ambition signals China's push to develop both memory and advanced packaging capabilities domestically. RMB 9B earmarked for HBM + AP suggests China is targeting not just DRAM production but the full HBM supply chain (stack assembly, TSV, base die integration).
