As ASML drives the transition from 6-inch to 12-inch photomasks for High-NA EUV systems, adoption roadmaps among major memory players are coming into focus.

**Samsung:**
- Targeting High-NA EUV for DRAM production by 2028.
- South Korea's first High-NA EUV system installed March 2025.
- Applying High-NA EUV to advanced DRAM could further enhance HBM performance — HBM4 development started with performance targets above international standards using its most advanced 1c-class DRAM process.

**SK hynix:**
- Also planning High-NA EUV into DRAM process, mass production by 2028.
- Considering joining the **Large Size Mask Consortium** led by TSMC.
- 1c DRAM: ~13% of DRAM bit mix in Q2'26 → projected 34% by Q4'26.
- 1c DRAM serves as core memory die for HBM4E (mass production 2027).

**Micron:**
- Relatively quiet; introduced first EUV-based 16Gb DDR5 with 1γ (1-gamma) process in 2025.
- 1γ expected to be majority of DRAM bit mix by mid-2026.
- Plans to expand EUV at 1δ (1-delta) node using "latest-generation EUV tools" for cleanroom efficiency.
- Recruiting for "High NA EUV Track – Equipment Engineer" at NY Creates site (Albany, NY) — Micron/IBM/AMAT/TEL joint High-NA EUV Center.

**Technical context:**
- ASML High-NA EUV (EXE series): NA = 0.55 vs 0.33 for existing NXE EUV.
- High-NA EUV can halve the number of multipatterning steps vs standard EUV, significantly reducing manufacturing costs.
- ASML 12-inch photomask pilot line: target 2031.

**Geopolitical dimension:**
- China has reportedly begun limited production of HBM3E using conventional processes.
- Samsung and SK hynix's push toward High-NA EUV is partly to maintain a technology lead over China's memory ambitions.
