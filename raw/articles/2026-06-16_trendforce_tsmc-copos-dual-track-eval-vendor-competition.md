# TSMC Reportedly Runs Dual-Track Evaluation on CoPoS Pilot Line, Sparking Global vs Local Vendor Competition

**Source**: TrendForce, 2026-06-16
**URL**: https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/06/16/news-tsmc-reportedly-runs-dual-track-evaluation-on-copos-pilot-line-sparking-global-vs-local-vendor-competition/

## Summary

TSMC's CoPoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate, Panel-Scale) pilot line at its Longtan facility is operating under a dual-track evaluation structure: one track led by major global equipment vendors and one track adopting solutions from Taiwanese local equipment makers. Both tracks compete on process stability, delivery times, cost efficiency, and local support.

## Key Data

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Pilot Line Location | Longtan facility (TSMC) |
| Panel Size | 310×310 mm |
| Mini Production Line | VisEra, 2026 |
| Trial Volume | 2027 |
| Mass Production Target | 2028–2029 |

## Global Equipment Track
Applied Materials, Tokyo Electron, SCREEN, DISCO, Lintec, Nitto, Yamada, Tazmo, KLA, Camtek

## Local (Taiwanese) Equipment Track
Gudeng, Mirle, Scientech, GPTC, Utechzone, VisEra, GPM

## Competitive Context
Samsung Electronics is advancing panel-level packaging with 415×510 mm format panels; Korean suppliers GigaVis, Semes, and Hanwha Semitech are also entering the PLP equipment market.

## Significance
The dual-track structure signals TSMC is simultaneously hedging supply chain risk and seeding a local Taiwanese equipment ecosystem for CoPoS—a strategic move given geopolitical pressure to localize advanced packaging. The 310×310 mm panel size is now confirmed for the pilot line, consistent with earlier SCHMID reports.
