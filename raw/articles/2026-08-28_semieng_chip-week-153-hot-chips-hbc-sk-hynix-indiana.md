## Advanced Packaging Highlights

### SK hynix Indiana Groundbreaking
SK hynix broke ground on its advanced packaging and R&D facility in West Lafayette, Indiana — the company's **first U.S. high-bandwidth memory (HBM) production hub**. SK hynix also signed a new advanced-packaging R&D agreement with **Purdue University**.

### Qualcomm High Bandwidth Compute (HBC)
Qualcomm stated that its **High Bandwidth Compute (HBC)** technology can address the AI memory wall by placing a compute die beneath a **3D-stacked LPDDR array on a plain organic substrate**, directly challenging the HBM-plus-interposer (CoWoS) approach. No silicon interposer needed.
- Source: Qualcomm at Deutsche Bank 2026 Technology Conference
- Counterpoint Research covered the strategic implications

### NVIDIA NVHBM
Nvidia expanded NVLink Fusion with **NVHBM**, a next-gen HBM technology that integrates a **custom memory controller into the 3D HBM stack** instead of the XPU (GPU die).

### Mitsubishi Chemical M-Filleris NTE (Warpage Material)
Mitsubishi Chemical is commercializing **M-Filleris NTE**, a **negative-thermal-expansion (NTE) filler** designed to reduce warpage in advanced semiconductor packages as conventional silica-loading approaches reach practical limits.
- Pilot sales: end of fiscal year 2026
- Mass production: second half of fiscal year 2027
- Purpose: warpage reduction in advanced packages (complements CTE mismatch management)

### Nordson ASYMTEK Vantage XL
Nordson introduced the **ASYMTEK Vantage XL**, a precision fluid-dispensing system designed for larger panel formats used in **panel-level and other advanced packaging applications**. Launched at SEMICON Taiwan 2026.

### Multi-Die Assemblies Becoming Standard
"Multi-die assemblies and heterogeneous integration are becoming standard in leading-edge servers and high-end edge devices, forcing broad changes in long-established design and manufacturing processes."

### Microsoft Maia 200 (Hot Chips 2026)
Microsoft revealed details on its **Maia 200** advanced AI accelerator:
- Process node: 3nm
- Memory: 216 GB HBM3E
- Performance: >10 PFLOPS FP4
- For inference workloads

### Hot Chips 2026 Other Notable
- Apple debuted 2nm M6 SoC and M5 Ultra
- Cerebras CS-4: ultrafast frontier inference (Nexus reusable rack-scale platform)
- OpenAI Jalapeño: shed light on performance vs rivals (not for commercial sale)
- Xcena MX1: computational CXL memory (memory expansion + near-memory computing)
- Samsung LPDDR5X-PIM findings presented

### Academic Research
- UM-Dearborn: Generative design for liquid-cooling channels in 2.7 kW multi-chip package (2 GPUs + 1 CPU) — G1016 reduces max GPU temp 33.6%, temp spread 52.5%, pressure drop 72.8%
- Georgia Tech: Cross-layer analysis of wafer-scale optical interconnects for MoE workloads

- Kioxia + SanDisk: >$31B Japan NAND investment through 2032; Kioxia Fab3 at Kitakami (BiCS 3D flash, FY2029)
