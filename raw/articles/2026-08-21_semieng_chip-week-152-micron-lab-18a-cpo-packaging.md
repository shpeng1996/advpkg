# Chip Industry Week In Review #152

**Published:** 2026-08-21 | **Source:** Semiconductor Engineering

**Summary:** New Micron lab; chip/AI price jump; multi-die monitoring; cryo links for quantum chips; interconnect RC bottleneck; 18A win; CPO roadmap; 2 new CEOs; humanoid readiness gap; Hot Interconnects.

---

## Sticker Shock

- **Gartner**: AI inferencing costs per agentic workflow will increase **fivefold** through 2028, with the rate of innovation outpacing the cost curve.
- **Samsung** increased pricing on new **4nm, 5nm, and 8nm** foundry orders by up to **15%** (Reuters). Its Pyeongtaek 4nm line is reportedly running at full capacity. **SMIC** also raised prices per last week's earnings call.

## New Offerings

- **Cerebras** introduced a rack-scale system containing three of its latest Wafer Scale Engine chips, which each provide up to **250 PFLOPS** of AI compute with **43.2 PB/s** of memory bandwidth.
- **proteanTecs** unveiled a suite of health, power, and performance management solutions for **multi-die systems that integrates per-chiplet visibility** from pre-assembly screening through SiP production and in-mission operation. (Key advanced packaging relevance: enabling monitoring and analytics across the full packaging lifecycle.)

## Deals

- **Silvaco** and **Dassault Systèmes** will co-develop interoperable **digital twin workflows** for semiconductor manufacturing.
- **Socionext** added **Intel 18A-P** to its custom-SoC roadmap — starting with a high-performance compute chiplet for AI and data centers. Another external customer win for Intel Foundry's leading-edge process.
- **Marvell** will develop custom silicon for **Google**'s TPU ecosystem (inference accelerators, NICs, memory interface controllers). Google can buy up to **$12.2B** worth of Marvell shares.
- **Nvidia** plans to invest **$1.5B** in SoftBank's SB Energy, which will build 8 GW data center in Ohio exclusively hosting Nvidia chips for OpenAI.

## CPO Roadmap

- Global researchers including **U. Virginia, MIT, SK hynix, UIUC, and Yonsei** published a **roadmap for co-packaged optics (CPO) for HPC and AI** in Nature Electronics (article doi: s41928-026-01681-6). This is the same study referenced in the SK hynix CPO/Nature Electronics coverage from 2026-08-20.

## Big Hauls

- **Groq LLC**: $350M for AI inference cloud.
- **Etched**: $700M for frontier model inference rack-scale system (co-designed chips, packages, PCBs, cold plates, interconnects). Follows $300M in July.
- **Broadcom**: reportedly seeking **$60B in debt funding** to finance an AI chip deal (Bloomberg).

## Research Programs

- **National Security Science and Technology Strategy** (White House 2026-08): directs federal agencies to prioritize semiconductor R&D spanning **heterogeneous integration and advanced packaging**, EDA, equipment/processes, AI/RF/optical hardware, 2D materials, integrated photonics.
- **Micron** plans to spend **$10B over 10 years** on a new **Micron Research Labs** in Boise, Idaho. Research areas: memory technologies, advanced memory and compute architectures, **packaging**, and manufacturing.
- **SRC** launched **+$13M SPARC** (industry-driven project-based university research) spanning design, manufacturing, and packaging.
- **UT Austin** researchers: **'Chips For Science'** report calling for broader access to advanced packaging facilities for universities, national labs and other researchers. Based on 70+ expert inputs; argues expanded packaging infrastructure needed to capitalize on U.S. semiconductor manufacturing investments.

## New Technologies — Advanced Packaging Relevant

- **JCET** fabricated samples of its **11.3:1 high-aspect-ratio TSV** technology. (JCET already in wiki — this adds new TSV specs.)
- **Synopsys** validated a **3D PCIe 6.0 PHY test chip** delivering up to **128.0 GB/s** through 8-lane PAM4 config.
- **Vuzix** started sampling a **waveguide bridge** for CPO/NPO interconnects in AI data centers.

## Research

- **NUS** researchers demonstrated **0.8-nm amorphous carbon** with dielectric constant **k=1.35**, high breakdown strength and metal-ion barrier properties — targeting advanced interconnects, CMOS scaling, and **monolithic 3D integration**.
- **UT Dallas** closed-loop digital twin for IGZO oxide-semiconductor transistors + 1T-1C DRAM optimization (Bayesian active learning).
- **Coherent** started sampling **300mm high thermal conductivity SiC substrates** for AI infrastructure.

## Earnings

- Keysight +36.5% YoY

## Hot Interconnects (Conference)

- **Arista** demonstrated 12.8-Tb/s, 64-channel liquid-cooled XPO optical module at 212 Gb/s/channel, ~130W total power.
