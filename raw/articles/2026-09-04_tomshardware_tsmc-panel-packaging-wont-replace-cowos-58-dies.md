# TSMC says panel packaging won't replace CoWoS anytime soon for the largest future AI processors

By Anton Shilov | September 4, 2026 | Tom's Hardware

## Key Claims

TSMC has clarified the relationship between its wafer-level CoWoS packaging and panel-level CoPoS (Chip-on-Panel-on-Substrate) technology:

**CoWoS can scale to 58 massive dies in one package** using wafer-level processes — a scale not achievable with panel-level approaches for the foreseeable future.

**Panel-level packaging (CoPoS) is complementary, not a replacement** for the highest-end AI accelerator packages. CoPoS targets a different market segment: large-scale integration at lower cost where the absolute maximum compute density is not required.

## CoWoS Scalability

- CoWoS scales all the way to **>14× reticle size** using wafer-level processes (as confirmed at North American Technology Symposium 2026)
- A 14-reticle CoWoS interposer can host up to **24 3D-stacked compute chiplets + 24 HBM5E stacks** by 2029
- Wafer-level precision enables tighter die-to-die interconnect pitches compared to panel-level approaches
- Panel-level processing introduces dimensional non-uniformity at scale that limits achievable interconnect pitches

## CoPoS vs CoWoS: Use Cases

| | CoWoS | CoPoS |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Highest-end AI accelerators | Mid-range AI, cost-sensitive HPC |
| Panel size | N/A (wafer) | 310mm × 310mm |
| Scale-up limit | 58+ dies in one package | Larger area, lower precision |
| Status | 5.5× reticle in HVM; 14× by 2028 | 2H28-29 HVM (C.C. Wei confirmed) |
| Next milestone | >14× reticle by 2029 | Glass core substrate (2030+) |

## Implications

TSMC's position clarifies the competitive landscape: panel-level packaging is NOT a near-term threat to CoWoS as the primary packaging technology for NVIDIA, AMD, and Google's highest-end AI chips. CoPoS and FOPLP are positioned for volume/cost applications where interconnect density requirements are lower.

This distinction is critical for understanding the OSAT opportunity: while Powertech's PiFO FOPLP (AMD+Broadcom, 2027) and ASE's FOCoS compete in the mid-market, TSMC's CoWoS maintains its stranglehold on frontier AI packaging.
