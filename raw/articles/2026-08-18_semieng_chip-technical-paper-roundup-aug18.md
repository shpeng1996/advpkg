## Key Packaging-Relevant Papers

### ReVolt: PDN-Aware Voltage Droop Control for 2.5D PIM Chiplet Architectures
- Authors: Washington State University + University of Wisconsin–Madison
- Published: August 17, 2026
- Topic: Power delivery network (PDN) challenges in 2.5D chiplet assemblies with processing-in-memory (PIM) architectures. Addresses voltage droop in heterogeneous integration scenarios.

### FAPlace: Joint Optimization of Chiplet Placement and Interposer Footprint for 2.5D Systems
- Authors: A*STAR (Singapore)
- Published: August 10, 2026
- Topic: Automated optimization framework for chiplet placement and interposer footprint sizing in 2.5D systems. Relevant to CoWoS and EMIB design automation.

### Innovative FA Hardware Solution for System-Level Debug of 3D ICs
- Authors: Google + Delft University of Technology (TU Delft)
- Published: August 10, 2026
- Topic: Failure analysis hardware tools enabling system-level debug of 3D integrated circuits. Relevant to HBM + compute die stacked assemblies.
