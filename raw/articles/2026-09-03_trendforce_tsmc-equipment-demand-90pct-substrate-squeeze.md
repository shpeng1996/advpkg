# TSMC Equipment Demand Reportedly Jumps 90% in Just Over Half a Year; Substrate Capacity Faces AI Squeeze

**Published**: 2026-09-03 | **Source**: TrendForce (citing Liberty Times, Economic Daily News, TechNews)

AI demand is pushing TSMC's capacity requirements to unprecedented levels. According to Liberty Times, TSMC Senior Vice President and Co-COO **Cliff Hou** said he has never seen demand grow at such a rapid pace and frequency in the past 30 years.

## Key Quotes and Data

- **Equipment demand**: stood at 1× at end of 2025, climbed to **1.9× by July 2026** — a **90% increase in just over half a year**
- Cliff Hou described the pace as posing a "major challenge for Taiwan's semiconductor ecosystem"
- TSMC is simultaneously constructing **13 fabs in Taiwan** and another **5–6 worldwide** (~20 total)
- Previous maximum: only 4–5 fabs under construction at a time
- Even with ~20 fabs, Hou said: **"still not enough to meet demand"**

## AI Demand Drivers

- AI's growing appetite for computing power and energy efficiency is accelerating demand for:
  - Advanced technologies
  - **Advanced packaging**
  - System-level performance
- Required a new model of industry collaboration
- Semiconductor production is no longer linear: companies must ensure performance achieved at one stage can be fully utilized by the next partner while maintaining manufacturability and yield — requiring much closer **supply-chain collaboration**

## Substrate Capacity Squeeze (Unimicron)

At the same SEMICON Taiwan 2026 event, **Unimicron Chairman S.C. Chien** highlighted:
- Severe supply-demand imbalance in semiconductor substrate market
- Requirements for **larger substrates, higher layer counts, more complex designs** (lower CTE, lower dielectric constants) — raising technical barriers
- Key production tools and materials (ABF, TGV-related) heavily reliant on **small and medium-sized Japanese suppliers**
- Unimicron has held **>12 in-depth meetings** with customers and Japanese suppliers in past 18 months
- Advanced and mature technologies are "highly complementary" in the AI era
- Mature nodes have new opportunities: high-density bonding, specialty memory, **silicon interposers with deep-trench capacitors**, silicon photonics
