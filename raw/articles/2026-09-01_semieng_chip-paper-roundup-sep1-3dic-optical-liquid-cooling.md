New technical papers recently added to Semiconductor Engineering's library (Sep 1, 2026):

## Packaging / Heterogeneous Integration Papers

**1. Open-Source Benchmark Suite of 3D-IC Testcases**
- Institution: UCLA
- Relevance: Provides standardized benchmarks for 2.5D/3D heterogeneous integration research in physical design — enables reproducible comparison of 3D-IC layout tools and algorithms.

**2. Generative Design of Liquid-Cooling Channels for Thermal Management of 2.5D and 3D Integrated Advanced Packaging**
- Institution: University of Michigan-Dearborn
- Note: Previously indexed (arXiv 2608.22787); -33.6% GPU temperature, -72.8% pressure drop results confirmed in SemiEng platform.

**3. Thermal Tuning Overhead in Wafer-Scale Optical Interconnects for LLM MoE Training: A Cross-Layer Analysis and Ferroelectric-Based Mitigation**
- Institution: Georgia Tech
- Relevance: Addresses thermal-optical coupling challenge in wafer-scale photonic interconnects for AI training. Ferroelectric tuning as mitigation strategy — directly relevant to TSMC COUPE and CPO thermal co-design challenges.
