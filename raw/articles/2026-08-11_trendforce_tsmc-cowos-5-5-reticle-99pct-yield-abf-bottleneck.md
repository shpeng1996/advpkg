# TSMC's 5.5-Reticle CoWoS Reportedly Tops 99% Yield; Flags Memory, ABF as AI Bottlenecks

**Source:** TrendForce | **Date:** 2026-08-11 | **Speaker:** Jun He (TSMC VP, Advanced Packaging Technology and Services) at 2026 OCP APAC Summit

## Core Announcements

### CoWoS Yield Milestone
- TSMC's **5.5-reticle-size CoWoS** is in volume production
- Yields consistently topping **98%** across multiple AI customer products
- Reaching as high as **99%** in some cases

### CoWoS Roadmap (reaffirmed)
- 14× reticle size by 2028 (~10 large compute dies + 20 HBM stacks)
- Beyond 14× reticles by 2029
- CoWoS capacity nearly doubled each year for the past three years
- TSMC now operates **10 advanced packaging facilities**
- "We haven't fully met demand yet, but we're getting very close" — Jun He

### SoIC Advances
- SoIC hybrid bonding: 50× higher interconnect density, 5× greater energy efficiency
- Pitch: 6µm (2025) → **4.5µm (2029)**
- A14-to-A14 SoIC available for production in **2029**, providing 1.8× higher die-to-die I/O density vs N2-on-N2 SoIC

### AI Supply Chain Bottlenecks Identified
- **Memory shortages** remain primary bottleneck
- **ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film) substrate** supply flagged as emerging bottleneck alongside memory
- Multiple sourcing being pursued but mechanical/thermal property variations between suppliers complicate process control
- ABF substrate tight supply expected "over the next few years"

### Development Cycle Acceleration
- TSMC plans validation scope publication **one year before mass production**
- System integrators, equipment makers, and materials suppliers brought into **parallel development** (6 quarters ahead of production)
- For CoWoS: development cycle reduced from ~**2 years/generation → 1 year**, shortening overall timeline by up to **three quarters**

## Competitive Context
- Intel EMIB-T: enables >6× reticle today, scaling >8× this year, >12× by 2028
- TSMC CoWoS 5.5× current volume production at 99% yield vs Intel EMIB scaling roadmap
