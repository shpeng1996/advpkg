Several semiconductor facilities in Kumamoto, Japan, including TSMC's JASM fab, suspended operations after a magnitude 7.1 earthquake struck the region on July 28, 2026.

## Restart Timeline

- **Tokyo Electron (TEL)**: Resumed operations at Kumamoto facilities (Koshi City and Ozu Town, Tokyo Electron Kyushu subsidiary) on August 3. No major damage to buildings or equipment found; limited business performance impact.
- **Sony Semiconductor Solutions**: Production at Kumamoto Technology Center (Kikuyo Town) to gradually restart from August 4; expected to recover to pre-earthquake levels by mid-August.
- **Renesas**: Nishiki Plant (Nishiki Town) resumed July 29 evening; Kawajiri Plant (Kumamoto City) to resume August 5.

## TSMC JASM Status

- **JASM Fab 2** (under construction): Construction work resumed after the earthquake.
- **JASM Fab 1**: Still temporarily halted for equipment inspections as of August 3. No concrete restart timeline announced. TSMC stated (July 29) that strong shaking requires additional time for adjustments and calibration. Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs confirmed TSMC has deployed resources to support JASM.

## Context

TSMC's Kumamoto JASM Fab 1 produces 22/28nm automotive and image sensor chips (Sony-related). This earthquake event highlights supply-chain risk concentration in Kumamoto prefecture, where TSMC, TEL, Sony, and Renesas are all co-located. Impact on advanced packaging supply chain is indirect (JASM Fab 1 is not an advanced packaging facility), but TEL equipment delays could propagate to broader packaging tool deliveries.
