Applied Materials raised its 2026 advanced-packaging equipment revenue growth forecast to above 70% (from a prior estimate of >50% given at Q2 reporting in May), driven by accelerating investment in HBM stacking and 3D chiplet assembly.

## Key Findings from Q3 FY2026 Earnings (reported ~August 14, 2026)

Applied Materials reported another record-breaking quarter in Q3 FY2026:
- Highest sequential revenue growth in company history for Q3
- Advanced-packaging equipment business: >70% revenue growth in FY2026 (prior estimate was >50% from Q2)
- Key growth drivers: HBM packaging equipment demand, 3D chiplet stacking systems
- China remains the top geographic market in Q3 (consistent with prior quarters)
- DRAM set to accelerate in 2H 2026 as capacity investment ramps

## Packaging Equipment Growth Context

Applied Materials' packaging equipment portfolio includes:
- Opta CMP (hybrid bonding dedicated, launched June 2026)
- Nokota VMax2 ECD (electrochemical deposition for Cu interconnects)
- Producer Avila2 PECVD (dielectric deposition for packaging)
- VeritySEM 7AP and SEMVision G7AP (advanced packaging inspection)
- The Kinex hybrid bonding platform (co-developed with Besi; Applied Materials holds 9% of Besi)

## Market Context

The upgrade from >50% to >70% packaging equipment growth in one quarter reflects:
1. Accelerating HBM4/HBM4E capacity buildout at Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron
2. TSMC SoIC hybrid bonding ramp (SoIC-X at 6µm, targeting 4.5µm by 2029)
3. Broader chiplet stacking adoption across AI and HPC applications
4. New products (AMAT launched 6 packaging equipment systems in late June 2026)
