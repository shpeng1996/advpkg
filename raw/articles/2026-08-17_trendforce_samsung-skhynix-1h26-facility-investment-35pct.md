Samsung and SK hynix are accelerating semiconductor investment as AI demand drives capacity expansion and advanced-process upgrades. According to Etoday, citing the companies' 2026 semi-annual reports, Samsung Electronics and SK hynix invested a combined KRW 43.198 trillion in semiconductor facilities in 1H26, up 35.1% YoY from KRW 31.9751 trillion.

**Samsung DS Division**: KRW 25.603 trillion (+23.5% YoY), focusing on next-generation memory technologies, advanced-process capacity expansion and infrastructure.

**SK hynix**: KRW 17.595 trillion (+56.4% YoY), stepped up spending to meet growing AI memory demand for HBM, high-capacity DRAM, and enterprise SSDs (eSSDs).

Both companies were effectively running at 100% utilization in 1H26 (Samsung DS memory lines and SK hynix).

## R&D Spending Surge

- **Samsung Electronics company-wide R&D**: KRW 27.363 trillion (+51.5% YoY) — record high for a six-month period
  - Note: separate DS division R&D not disclosed
- **SK hynix R&D**: KRW 6.043 trillion (+98.4% YoY) — approximately 90% of full-year 2025 R&D spending

## Micron Ventures Paradigm Fund

Micron launched its US$250 million Micron Ventures Paradigm Fund, its largest venture fund to date, targeting AI model architectures, computing, enterprise applications, and physical AI. Aimed at identifying future memory and storage requirements early and building partnerships for next-generation product development.

## Customer Mix Shifts as AI Chip Competition Expands

**SK hynix customer mix:**
- NVIDIA generated KRW 17.6087 trillion in 1H26 = 13.35% of total revenue
- Down from NVIDIA's ~24% share of full-year 2025 revenue
- Attributed to greater customer diversification toward Big Tech companies developing their own AI chips (ASICs)
- NVIDIA's share could rebound in 2H26 as SK hynix ramps HBM4 for Vera Rubin platform

**Samsung major customers 1H26 (five largest, ~25% total revenue):**
1. Alphabet (Google)
2. Amazon
3. Apple
4. Hong Kong Techtronics
5. Supreme Electronics

⚠️ **NVIDIA was NOT among Samsung's top five customers in 1H26** — a significant signal about Samsung HBM qualification status. Samsung's HBM cooperation with NVIDIA remains closely watched, while Alphabet, Amazon, and Apple currently represent a larger share of Samsung's actual revenue.
