The glass substrate race is heating up as AI chips demand larger packages. Japan's Shinko Electric Industries has developed a 22-layer glass substrate by stacking 11 copper wiring layers on each side, addressing the "SeWaRe" issue of internal cracking or delamination.

The shift toward larger AI packages is exposing warpage limits of conventional organic substrates. Glass offers greater rigidity and less thermal deformation.

Shinko Electric showcased at Advanced Packaging Summit 2026 (July). The company reinforced substrate edges with resin and applied edge-protection material to suppress SeWaRe defects under thermal loading. Result: 22-layer glass-core build-up substrate with TGV technology.

Japan's DNP (Dai Nippon Printing) launched a TGV glass substrate pilot line in Saitama in December 2025, targeting mass production in 2028.

Japanese players appear to have technological edge over South Korean peers in glass substrates.

Samsung Electro-Mechanics is accelerating its push via GlaSSEM JV (with Dongwoo Fine-Chem, Korean subsidiary of Sumitomo Chemical). Target: FC-BGA package substrates for AI semiconductors. Customer validation underway.

However, ramp-up timeline is uncertain. GlaSSEM has yet to provide equipment suppliers with a firm ordering schedule. Timeline pushed back at least 3 times: December 2025 -> March -> June 2026. Delays may be linked to failed reliability qualification at a global communications chip customer (per The Elec). Production-line construction may not begin until 2H27; mass production 2028 or later.

Samsung EM official statement (2026-08-19): "No failed customer qualification; evaluation ongoing; target 2H27 for glass core material, 2028 for finished glass substrates."

Key data points:
- Shinko Electric: 22-layer glass-core substrate (11 Cu layers per side); TGV; SeWaRe solution via edge-resin reinforcement
- DNP: Saitama TGV pilot line launched December 2025; HVM target 2028
- GlaSSEM timeline: Dec 2025 -> Mar -> Jun 2026 (3+ delays); 2H27 production line; 2028+ HVM
- Samsung EM official target: 2H27 glass core material, 2028 finished glass substrates
