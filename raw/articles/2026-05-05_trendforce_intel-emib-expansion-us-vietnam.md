# Intel Reportedly Scales EMIB Expansion in U.S., Vietnam, with Taiwanese Tool Orders Set for 2H26 Delivery

**Published**: May 5, 2026 | **Source**: TrendForce

---

As Intel's EMIB (Embedded Multi-die Interconnect Bridge) advanced packaging gains traction and draws strong market attention, the company is reportedly accelerating capacity expansion globally.

## U.S. and Vietnam Expansion

Intel is advancing EMIB capacity expansion in parallel, with its **Oregon facility in the U.S. taking the lead**, while **Vietnam** has been positioned as another key growth hub.

Intel has already placed substantial equipment orders with Taiwanese suppliers. Beneficiaries are said to include:
- **E&R Engineering Corp.** (laser equipment)
- **C Sun** (dry-process specialist)
- **AblePrint** (curing equipment)

Deliveries are scheduled for the **second half of 2026** (2H26).

## EMIB Yield Milestone

According to Wccftech, Intel's EMIB has reached **~90% yield levels**, with **Google and Meta Platforms** emerging as potential adopters for future designs. EMIB-T scales to >12× reticle by 2028.

## Vietnam Strategy

Intel is relocating parts of its assembly, packaging, and testing operations from Costa Rica to the **Saigon Hi-Tech Park (SHTP)** in Ho Chi Minh City, where production will focus on chips for data center servers.

Intel Vietnam at SHTP has:
- Total committed investment of **US$4.115 billion** (~30% of the park's total registered capital)
- 46.6-hectare site
- Produced more than **3.8 billion units** to date
- Products include: Lunar Lake, Arrow Lake, Battlemage graphics
- Intel Vietnam supports advanced Foveros 3D packaging, 5G products, discrete graphics
- Future products include **Panther Lake and Wildcat Lake** (both based on Intel's cutting-edge 18A process)

## Context

Intel's leading-edge nodes (spearheaded by 18A) are gradually building momentum alongside its advanced packaging portfolio. Intel has also outsourced EMIB production for the first time to **Amkor's Songdo K5 facility** in South Korea, with additional sites planned in Portugal and Arizona.

Intel's Penang advanced packaging complex in Malaysia is reportedly 99% complete, with first-phase operations beginning in 2026.
