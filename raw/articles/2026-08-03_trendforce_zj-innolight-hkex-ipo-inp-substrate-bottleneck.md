Zhongji Innolight (ZJ Innolight), a global leader in optical transceivers, officially listed on the Main Board of HKEX on July 30, 2026. IPO priced at HKD 980/share, base gross proceeds HKD 53.41 billion (~US$6.87B). If 15% over-allotment exercised: HKD 61.42 billion total. Largest HKEX IPO in 2026 to date; largest HK listing since Alibaba 2019 (7-year record). 33 cornerstone investors including Temasek, Blackstone, ADIA, Hillhouse, Alibaba, Tencent. Cornerstone investors hold ~49.12% of global offering.

## Capital Allocation

- ~30% (~HKD 16.35B): Global capacity expansion — +50M high-end optical modules/year over 3 years; target 1.6T+ products
- ~30%: Next-gen R&D — 1.6T, 2.4T, 3.2T modules; CPO and NPO architectures
- ~15%: Strategic M&A — vertical integration in optical chips, components, raw materials
- ~25%: Supply chain resilience — strategic reserves for III-V semiconductor substrates and laser chips

## InP Substrate Bottleneck

As 1.6T optical transceivers enter mass production, supply-demand squeeze intensifies for Indium Phosphide (InP) substrates — essential for high-end laser chips.

Global capacity expansion response:
- **Coherent**: launched 6-inch InP wafer expansion in Texas (June 2026); capacity to quadruple; backed by USD 2B long-term agreement with NVIDIA
- **AXT**: planning to double InP capacity by end-2027

Chinese localized supply chain:
- **Yunnan Germanium**: High-quality InP single-crystal wafer project (CNY 189M, April 2026); +300K InP wafers/year → total 450K wafers/year
- **Vital Materials/Guangdong Xianrui**: 40-ton/year InP crystal project launched July 21, 2026; EIA completed
- **Tianjin Kuanjing Semiconductor**: 6-inch InP single-crystal industrialization project (signed June 24, 2026); facility delivered July 2026
