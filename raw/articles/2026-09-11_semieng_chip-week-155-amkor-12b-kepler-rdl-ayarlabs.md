# Chip Industry Week in Review (Week #155)

**Published:** September 11, 2026 | **Source:** Semiconductor Engineering

**Summary:** 12-inch high-NA EUV masks; Arm's edge AI push; Amkor's expansion; 3D memory; FeRAM startup makes waves; Qualcomm-AWS $60B custom silicon deal; Ayar Labs' scale-up deal; new iPhone chip and format; rogue OpenAI agents in Germany; fab factory; fine-pitch RDL tech for advanced packaging.

---

## SPIE Photomask Technology + EUV Lithography Conference

- **ASML + TSMC** launched industry initiative to transition high-NA EUV from 6-inch to **12-inch photomasks**; targeting 12-inch mask pilot line by 2031, production-ready by 2033; TSMC begins high-NA EUV HVM in 2030.
- eBeam Initiative survey: 66% expect large-format photomask adoption within a decade; 94% expect mask revenue to grow in 2026.
- imec demonstrated single-exposure high-NA EUV patterning of 22nm-pitch lines/spaces and 28nm-pitch vias using chemically amplified resist (for A14/A10 logic nodes).
- Intel Foundry + ASML: high-NA EUV has processed >1 million wafers; select production layers for Panther Lake; Intel 18A high-NA layers meeting/exceeding conventional EUV patterns.
- Samsung formally joined 12-inch photomask initiative; plans high-NA EUV into DRAM HVM in 2028.

## New Capacity — Advanced Packaging

- **Amkor Phase 2 Arizona Advanced Packaging Campus** (announced 2026-09-08):
  - Total investment expanded to ~**$12B** (up from Phase 1 ~$7B)
  - Phase 2 adds **60,000 sqm cleanroom** (Phase 1: 33,000 sqm → total: ~93,000 sqm, nearly 3×)
  - Phase 2 construction starts late 2027; completion by end of 2029
  - Capabilities: wafer bump, probe, assembly and test
  - First OSAT HVM production site in the United States for advanced packaging
  - Total campus: 3,500+ Arizona employees
- **ASML** begins construction of new campus at Brainport Industries Campus North near Eindhoven (second major site for future manufacturing growth).

## Memory

- **SK hynix Future Forum 2026**: outlined strategy spanning HBM, 3D DRAM, HBF, CXL and SSDs; memory increasingly co-designed at system level for AI; identified **3D memory as a key future technology**.
- **Kepler Computing** emerged from stealth:
  - Raised **$468M**
  - New high-bandwidth AI memory architecture using **3D stacking + ferroelectric materials (FeRAM)**
  - Claims **order of magnitude capacity increase** vs SRAM and HBM
  - Production ramp targeted for **2027**
- Global DRAM industry revenue jumped **~60% QoQ to ~$155B** in Q2 2026 (TrendForce); market share: Samsung 39%, SK hynix 25%, Micron 23%.
- Chinese AI chip suppliers (**Huawei, Cambricon**) raising accelerator prices as HBM access is limited.

## New Technologies — Advanced Packaging

- **Taiyo Holdings + imec**: Demonstrated 3-layer RDL with **700nm wiring and vias on 300mm wafers** using Taiyo's new photosensitive dielectric material → advances fine-pitch RDL technology for advanced packaging.
- **Infineon TDA235E5/E0**: dual-phase smart power stage >2 A/mm² for next-gen AI accelerators and vertical power delivery modules.

## Big Deals

- **Qualcomm + Amazon** deal worth up to **$60B**: custom silicon for large-scale AI data centers + optical connectivity up to 1.6T + future solutions.
- **Analog Devices** acquires **Alif Semiconductor** for $1.35B (AI-native MCUs).
- **OpenAI + Samsung**: working on next-gen chips; significant progress in joint production and research.

## Fundings / CPO

- **Ayar Labs**: raised $150M (total: **$650M**) for CPO scale-up to HVM; **Wiwynn** strategic investment earlier in 2026. Focus: CPO for AI scale-up networking.
- **Positron AI**: $875M for memory-first inference silicon (commodity LPDDR5X-based).
- **Fab2**: $500M to mass-produce semiconductor fabs.
- **Celero Communications**: $275M for 1.6T→3.2T AI optical interconnects after validating 2nm coherent DSP silicon.
- **nanoSkunkWorkX**: $2M seed for **package copper for AI and HPC**.

## Smartphone Chips

- **Apple A20 Pro** (2nm, TSMC): iPhone 18 — 50% more memory bandwidth + integrated neural accelerators. Also: iPhone Duo (first Apple foldable).
- **Huawei Kirin 9050** (LogicFolding architecture): Mate XT 2 trifold; 42% performance improvement.

## Data Center Networking

- **d-Matrix** will use **NVIDIA NVLink Fusion** to connect Raptor inference processors into NVIDIA-based systems; Raptor final design 2026, rack-scale systems 2027.
- OIF published 1600ZR coherent interface IA (doubling capacity vs 800ZR).
