<!-- TSMC EMIB-Like silicon bridge packaging development -->

TSMC is reportedly developing a chip packaging technology similar to Intel's Embedded Multi-die Interconnect Bridge (EMIB), internally called **"EMIB Like"**, and is partnering with Taiwan's **Kinsus Interconnect Technology** on the project, per The Information (July 2026).

Key facts:
- TSMC internally calling the technology "EMIB Like"
- Partnership with Kinsus Interconnect Technology (Taiwan substrate maker)
- **NVIDIA** is evaluating Intel's EMIB packaging technology for a future processor
- If commercialized, would enable end-to-end TSMC service for chiplet integration

TSMC context:
- Currently uses CoWoS (silicon interposer) for AI chips for NVIDIA and AMD
- April 2026: TSMC announced plans for 14-reticle CoWoS package (production 2028) capable of integrating ~10 large compute dies and 20 HBM stacks
- EMIB-Like would be a local silicon bridge alternative to full-wafer interposer (cost reduction for certain configurations)

Intel context:
- Intel EMIB uses embedded silicon bridges to connect chip dies at high density without full interposer
- Intel-Lens Technology partnership for glass substrate-based advanced packaging
- Intel EMIB-T now in foundry-accessible form (external customers)
