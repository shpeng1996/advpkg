# Amkor Technology Announces Strategic Partnership with NVIDIA to Expand Advanced Packaging and Test for Next-Generation AI Infrastructure

**$1.5 Billion Multi-Year Advanced Packaging and Development Agreement**
Published: 2026-07-23 | Tempe, AZ | Business Wire

## Summary

Amkor Technology (Nasdaq: AMKR) announced a **multi-year strategic partnership with NVIDIA** to develop advanced semiconductor packaging and test technologies for next-generation AI and accelerated computing platforms. NVIDIA will provide a **prepayment to support expansion of Amkor's U.S. advanced packaging capacity**.

## Key Terms & Scope

- **Deal size**: $1.5 billion multi-year
- **Structure**: NVIDIA prepayment supporting Amkor U.S. capacity expansion
- **Geography**: Arizona (primary) + established Asia manufacturing footprint
- **Focus technologies**: High-density interconnects, next-generation heterogeneous integration
- **Scope**: Full turnkey advanced packaging and test solutions

## Technology & Products Covered

- Amkor has delivered advanced packaging for NVIDIA platforms including:
  - Data center processors
  - Networking chipsets
  - Next-generation accelerated computing systems
- New partnership aligns long-term roadmaps to bring new technologies to market at scale

## Strategic Rationale

**NVIDIA perspective** (Debora Shoquist, EVP Operations):
> "AI is driving a generational shift in technology, transforming every industry and creating a unique opportunity to reinvigorate American manufacturing and supply chains. Amkor's global capabilities, combined with their committed investment in the United States, are critical components of building resilient AI infrastructure."

**Amkor perspective** (Kevin Engel, CEO):
> "This strategic partnership with NVIDIA underscores the central role advanced packaging plays in enabling the future of AI. Our agreement with NVIDIA accelerates our long-term roadmap and supports our ability to deliver full turnkey advanced packaging and test solutions."

## Context & Significance

- Amkor stock jumped **+17% in extended trading** on announcement
- Complements Amkor's **10-year TSMC partnership** (announced June 2026) for Arizona CoWoS/InFO packaging
- Arizona campus: established footprint for $7B investment, production ~2028
- Creates a **geographically diverse supply chain** spanning Asia + United States
- Amkor is the **world's largest U.S.-headquartered OSAT**

## Industry Position

This agreement, combined with the TSMC-Amkor 10-year deal, positions Amkor's Arizona Peoria campus as the hub for U.S. AI chip advanced packaging — anchored by both the world's largest foundry and the world's most valuable AI chip company.
