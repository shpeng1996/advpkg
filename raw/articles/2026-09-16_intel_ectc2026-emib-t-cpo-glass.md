# How Intel Foundry Packaging Technologies Redefine AI and HPC Scalability Limits at ECTC 2026

First-party Intel Foundry summary of its **ECTC 2026** packaging programme — **20 technical papers**, including collaborations with **Siliconware Precision Industries (SPIL)**, **Fourier Scientific**, **NIST**, and **EV Group**.

## EMIB-T

Presented as enabling "ultra large, high-performance chiplet systems" by combining embedded silicon bridges for lateral 2.5D interconnect with **TSV vertical scaling**.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| First-layer interconnect bump pitch | **down to 25 µm** |
| Package form factor | **up to 120 × 120 mm** |
| Compute + memory silicon content | **more than 9× reticles** on a single package |
| Signal/power integrity | **over 12 Gb/s for HBM4E**; **64 Gb/s UCIe** |

## Co-packaged optics

Two innovations highlighted:

1. **High-performance detachable edge optical connector** — glass fan-out couplers plus expanded-beam edge connectors, for "low-loss, highly reliable, and detachable optical interfaces."
2. **V-groove based edge coupling glass coupler interface** — replaces the traditional fiber array unit (FAU) approach with rigid optical glass couplers.

## Glass core substrates

- **Through-glass vias (TGVs) with copper filling** surviving "very harsh thermal cycling without failure."
- Superior flatness and dimensional stability vs. organic substrates.
- Supports "electrical and optical integration on one platform."
