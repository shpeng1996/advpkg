# China's Semiconductor Sector Attracts Billions in Industrial Investment Funds

**2026-07-29 | TrendForce**

A new wave of industrial capital is moving into China's semiconductor sector, as local governments and industry leaders accelerate investments in strategic technologies. Recently, Shenzhen, Shanghai and Wuhan have unveiled a series of major industrial fund initiatives and registration updates.

## Key Fund Announcements

### Shenzhen: RMB 10 billion industrial ecosystem fund (July 20)
- **Shenzhen Major Investment No.1** fund completed AMAC registration
- Total size: **RMB 10 billion** (initial paid-in capital: RMB 3.94 billion)
- Dual GP management structure
- Focus: industrial software, high-end chip design, advanced manufacturing, **core semiconductor equipment, high-purity materials, and advanced packaging**
- Geographic scope: upstream/downstream collaboration within and beyond Shenzhen

### Wuhan Optics Valley: RMB 18 billion parent fund cluster
Four industrial parent funds with combined scale of RMB 18 billion:

| Fund | Size | Sponsor | Focus |
|------|------|---------|-------|
| Optoelectronic Information Industry | RMB 5B | Hubei Science and Technology Investment Group | Optical communications, laser |
| IC Fund #1 | RMB 5B | Wuhan Optics Valley Financial Holding | Memory chips, compound semiconductors, **advanced packaging** |
| IC Fund #2 | RMB 5B | Wuhan Optics Valley Financial Holding | Quantum technology, embodied intelligence, photonic integration |
| Life Sciences | RMB 3B | Wuhan Hi-tech Holding | Healthcare |

- Target by 2030: leverage RMB 18B cluster to attract sub-funds totaling **>RMB 100 billion**

## Leading Companies as LP Investors

### AMEC Research joins RMB 3 billion semiconductor fund (Shanghai)
- AMEC Lingang (wholly owned AMEC subsidiary) plans LP participation
- Fund: Shanghai Wisdom Lingfeng Venture Capital Partnership
- Target size: **RMB 3 billion**; AMEC Lingang commitment: up to RMB 1.47 billion (≤49%)
- GP: Wisdom Capital (≥RMB 30M)
- Focus: next-generation manufacturing technologies, broader semiconductor ecosystem

### USI (Universal Scientific Industrial) invests RMB 50 million
- SiP and EMS provider USI invests as LP in Shanghai Shangce Xingrongxin Fund
- Fund target: RMB 800 million; focus: integrated circuits and applications
- Strategic rationale: SiP convergence with chiplet architectures → upstream innovation visibility

## Analysis
These capital moves signal a more pragmatic approach in China's hard-tech investment landscape. Unlike earlier direct-equity strategies, leading technology companies are increasingly participating as core LPs in specialized private equity funds — leveraging external capital while nurturing upstream technologies and reducing balance-sheet exposure.

USI's participation is notable as SiP/system-level packaging converges with chiplet architectures, positioning downstream manufacturers for early visibility into advanced packaging technologies and materials.
