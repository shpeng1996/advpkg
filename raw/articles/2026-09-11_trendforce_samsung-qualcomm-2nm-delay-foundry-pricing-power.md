Samsung Electronics' efforts to bring Qualcomm back as a major foundry customer may face another delay. Sources say Qualcomm AP production using Samsung Foundry's 2nm could be pushed back to 2027, with prolonged price negotiations making production before end-2026 increasingly uncertain.

**Key findings:**

- The delay stems primarily from pricing differences, not technical issues. Samsung's 2nm process has largely stabilized.
- Samsung 2nm GAA yield has climbed to **over 70%**, up sharply from below 50% at the start of 2026.
- Qualcomm is seeking lower prices, but Samsung is now in a stronger position after securing major customers (Tesla, Broadcom), and evaluates contracts more selectively.

**Samsung Foundry pricing power:**

- Samsung raised prices in July 2026 for 4nm SF4 orders by **10–15%** (China and U.S. customers) and 5–10% (Taiwan customers).
- 5nm SF5 prices rose 10–15%; 8nm prices up ~10%.
- Strong AI chip demand and tight TSMC capacity strengthened Samsung's pricing power.

**Samsung Foundry first Taylor fab fully booked (before opening):**

- Orders linked to **Tesla AI5/AI6**, Broadcom next-gen comms chips, and Arm AI chips for smart devices.

**Samsung Foundry Q2 2026:**

- Ranked 2nd globally in foundry revenue; advanced-node orders including HBM base dies ramped up.
- Market share 5.9% (fell as rivals grew faster); third-ranked SMIC narrowing gap.

**Exynos 2700:**

- Set to use Samsung Foundry's second-generation 2nm SF2P process for Galaxy S27.
