# LG enters chip packaging arena with Laser Direct Imaging machine, as TSMC's CoWoS remains constrained

**Publisher:** Tom's Hardware | **Published:** 2026-08-25

## Key Facts

LG Electronics Production Technology Institute (LG-PRI) has signed a contract with an OSAT to supply a **maskless laser direct imaging (LDI) lithography tool** for semiconductor packaging.

### Technology

- **Purpose:** Pattern fine metal-interconnect layers in advanced packaging (RDL patterning)
- **Design philosophy:** Maskless — no photomask needed (unlike conventional stepper lithography)
- **Trade-off:** Resolution traded for higher throughput
- **Highest-resolution version:** 1.5-µm line-and-space patterns → wiring pitches of ~3 µm
- **Application:** CoWoS-type advanced packaging RDL layers

### Market Context

- TSMC's CoWoS remains capacity-constrained (supply-demand gap narrowing from ~20% to ~10% by end-2026, but still significant)
- LG-PRI's entry reflects growing equipment ecosystem around advanced packaging
- Maskless LDI can offer cost advantages (no mask cost) for lower-volume or rapid-iteration packaging lines
- Competes in the space occupied by Mycronic, Orbotech (KLA), and CFMEE (China's PLP 2000)

### Significance

- LG (traditionally consumer electronics / display) entering the semiconductor packaging equipment market
- Signals that packaging lithography is attracting new entrants beyond traditional semiconductor equipment makers
- CoWoS RDL patterning (currently ~2–5 µm pitch) is a bottleneck; new tools could ease capacity
