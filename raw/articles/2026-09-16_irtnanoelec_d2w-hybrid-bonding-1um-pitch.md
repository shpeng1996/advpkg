# Die-to-Wafer Hybrid Bonding At 1 μm Pitch, Removing Bottleneck for AI Hardware

CEA-Leti, within the IRT Nanoelec / FAMES Pilot Line programme, reports a **world-first die-to-wafer (D2W) hybrid bonding demonstration at 1 µm Cu-Cu interconnect pitch**, presented at **ECTC 2026**. Lead author: Melissa Najem, CEA-Leti research engineer.

## Key numbers

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Demonstrated pitch | **1 µm** (D2W, Cu-Cu) — stated as a world first |
| Roadmap target | **0.5 µm** pitch |
| Test structures | up to **100,000 links** electrically tested |
| Yield confirmed | **5 µm down to 2 µm** pitch |
| 1 µm yield limiter | **alignment accuracy of existing bonding tools** |
| Next-gen tool expectation | **0.5 µm (3σ) alignment** capability |

## Process building blocks

- Inter-die gap filling (IDGF)
- High-density through-silicon vias (HD TSV)
- Through-oxide vias (TOV)
- Chemical mechanical planarization (CMP)

## Why it matters (as stated by the source)

The technology "shortens interconnect paths, significantly increasing data transfer speeds while reducing power consumption" for AI accelerators.

## Funding / partners
