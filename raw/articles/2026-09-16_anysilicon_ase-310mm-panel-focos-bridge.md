# ASE Launches 310mm Panel-Level Packaging Line for AI and Chiplet Applications

ASE (Advanced Semiconductor Engineering), described in the piece as "the world's largest outsourced semiconductor assembly and test company," has launched a **310mm × 310mm panel-level packaging line** aimed at AI processors, HPC devices and chiplet-based architectures.

## Key specifications

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Panel format | 310 mm × 310 mm |
| Usable surface area | 96,100 mm² |
| Platforms supported | FOCoS (Fan-Out Chip-on-Substrate), FOCoS-Bridge |
| FOCoS RDL line/space | 2/2 µm |
| FOCoS-Bridge RDL line/space | 8/8 µm |

**FOCoS-Bridge** uses embedded silicon bridge structures for die-to-die chiplet interconnection, i.e. an OSAT-side analogue of the local-silicon-bridge approach rather than a full silicon interposer.

## Target applications

- AI processors
- High-performance computing (HPC) devices
- Chiplet-based architectures
