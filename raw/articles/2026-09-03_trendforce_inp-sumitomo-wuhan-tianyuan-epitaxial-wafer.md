The market for Indium Phosphide (InP), a critical material in optical communications, has recently seen significant developments. Japanese chemical giant Sumitomo Chemical announced the commercial mass production of 4-inch InP epitaxial wafers, targeting JPY 10 billion in sales by the mid-2030s. Concurrently, Chinese environmental services provider Wuhan Tianyuan acquired a 60% stake in Zhongxun Semiconductor for CNY 7.5 million to enter the InP substrate market, planning an additional CNY 67 million in supporting investments.

## Sumitomo Announces Mass Production of 4-Inch InP Epitaxial Wafers

On August 31, Sumitomo Chemical announced the establishment of a mass production system for **4-inch InP epitaxial wafers** at its Ibaraki Works in Hitachi, Ibaraki Prefecture, and officially commenced commercial sales. As one of the few pure-play foundries in Japan capable of stable InP epi-wafer mass production, the company expanded its capacity to address the technological trend of **Optoelectronic Convergence in AI data centers**.

Sumitomo boasts over 25 years of mass production experience in Gallium Arsenide (GaAs) and Gallium Nitride (GaN) epitaxial wafers. By transferring its proprietary composition control, thin-film stacking, and crystal growth process control technologies to InP epi-wafer production, the company achieved high uniformity in crystal structure, high mass-production reproducibility, and uniform film thickness.

Sumitomo plans to integrate InP epitaxial wafers into its existing compound semiconductor portfolio—which includes GaN substrates, GaN epi-wafers, and GaAs epi-wafers—with a target of reaching approximately **JPY 10 billion in sales by the mid-2030s**.

## Wuhan Tianyuan Acquires 60% Stake in Zhongxun Semiconductor for CNY 7.5 Million

In late August, Wuhan Tianyuan announced the completion of its acquisition of a 60% stake in **Zhongxun Semiconductor (Jiangsu) Co., Ltd.**, marking its official entry into the InP substrate sector.

- Acquisition consideration: CNY 7.5 million
- Additional planned investment: CNY 67 million (equipment, facilities, working capital)
- Total planned investment: approximately CNY 74.5 million

Zhongxun Semiconductor specializes in compound semiconductor substrates including InP. Holdings: 29 active patents. Trial production capabilities for 2-to-4-inch single-crystal InP substrates, with ~30% yield in single-crystal growth. Currently advancing 4-inch product commercial ramp-up and 6-inch substrate R&D. Sample shipments and parameter validations completed with several leading semiconductor clients. As of 1H26, no commercial revenue yet.

Wuhan Tianyuan was founded in 2009 as an environmental services company, but has since established a dual-core strategy of "Environmental Protection + Green Energy" while expanding into AI infrastructure and digital-intelligence applications. It has secured CNY 150 million in computing power center construction orders.
