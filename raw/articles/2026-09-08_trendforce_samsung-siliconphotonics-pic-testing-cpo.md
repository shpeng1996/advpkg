Samsung is bringing silicon photonics photonic integrated circuit (PIC) wafer testing in-house, using FormFactor and MPI probe stations — the same tools TSMC qualified for its COUPE CPO platform.

**Key findings:**

- Samsung previously outsourced PIC wafer validation to external orgs including imec; now establishing its own testing environment.
- **FormFactor and MPI** are the only probe station suppliers to have passed TSMC's equipment qualification for CPO-related testing.
- Samsung targeting completion of in-house PIC wafer testing by **end-2026**.
- Samsung's foundry business has secured an order from a major optical communications module maker; mass production begins **2H26**.
- Broadcom cited as a potential Samsung SiPh customer.

**Roadmap:**

- 2026: In-house PIC wafer testing
- 2027: Test EIC-PIC optical engines (EIC handles electrical, PIC handles optical signals; stacked together)
- 2027: Launch Samsung Foundry silicon photonics foundry services (PDKs for customers)
- Long-term: Integrate optical engines with logic chips and HBM; expand CPO from network switches to GPUs and CPUs

**Industry context:**

- TSMC COUPE CPO production starting 2H26 (confirmed).
- CPO switch market volume ramp expected 2027–2028.
- TrendForce: vertical integration (SiPh design + optical engine mfg + advanced packaging) is becoming the default CPO playbook.
- CPO testing remains a bottleneck: no unified standards, largely manual processes.
