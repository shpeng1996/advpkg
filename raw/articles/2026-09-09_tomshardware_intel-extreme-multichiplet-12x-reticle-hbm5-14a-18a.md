# Intel Displays Tech to Build Extreme Multi-Chiplet Packages 12x the Size of the Largest AI Processors

**Published:** ~September 2026 | **Source:** Tom's Hardware

## Key Concept: Intel "Extreme Multi-Chiplet" Vision

Intel Foundry has demonstrated engineering capability to build a theoretical extreme multi-chiplet package:
- **Scale:** 12× the size of the largest AI chips currently on market (beating TSMC's planned ~9.5× reticle CoWoS)
- **Floorplan:** Approximately the size of a cellphone
- **Components:**
  - ≥16 compute elements (AI engines or CPUs) on **Intel 14A** or 14A-E process (1.4nm-class, second-gen RibbonFET 2, improved PowerVia Direct backside power delivery)
  - 8 reticle-sized compute **base dies** on **18A-PT** (1.8nm-class, performance-enhanced, TSVs, backside power)
  - **24 HBM5** memory stacks
  - Interconnect: **EMIB-T** (Enhanced EMIB with TSVs) laterally + **UCIe-A** on top for die-to-die connections + **Foveros Direct 3D** bonding

## Key Technology Enablers

| Technology | Role | Status |
|-----------|------|--------|
| EMIB-T | Lateral multi-die interconnect with TSVs for power | Production ramp 2H 2026 |
| Foveros Direct 3D | Vertical hybrid bonding | HVM 2026 (Clearwater Forest) |
| 18A-PT | Base die process (performance + TSV enhanced) | 2027+ |
| 14A / 14A-E | Compute tile process (next after 18A) | 2027-2028 |
| UCIe-A | Advanced UCIe die-to-die links | On roadmap |

## Timeline Assessment

This extreme concept may emerge toward the end of the decade (2028–2030), when Intel has perfected:
- Foveros Direct 3D packaging technology
- 18A and 14A production nodes at volume
- EMIB-T at full scale

The package would represent ~12× reticle scaling vs TSMC's planned 14× reticle CoWoS (the largest known planned package). Intel is positioning EMIB-T as the path to out-scale TSMC's CoWoS in the long term.

## Context: Current EMIB-T Status (2026)

- Already supports packages up to 120×180mm
- >38 bridges, >12 reticle-sized dies
- Supports HBM3, HBM3E, HBM4, HBM5
- First external customer products expected 2027–2028
- Clearwater Forest (288-core Xeon 6+): 17-tile package, 12 bridges — internal production proof
