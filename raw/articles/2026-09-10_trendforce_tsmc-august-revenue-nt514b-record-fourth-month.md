# TSMC August Revenue Tops NT$500B for First Time, Sets Record for Fourth Straight Month

**Source:** TrendForce, citing TSMC press release, Economic Daily News, Central News Agency (2026-09-10)

## Key Financial Data

- **August 2026 Revenue**: NT$514.8 billion (+10.1% MoM, +53.3% YoY)
- **First time** monthly revenue exceeds NT$500 billion
- **4th consecutive monthly record high**
- **Jan–Aug 2026 total**: NT$3.39 trillion (+39.3% YoY)
- **Q3 2026 revenue guidance**: US$44.6B–US$45.8B (+12% QoQ at midpoint)
- **Full-year 2026 target** (C.C. Wei): USD revenue growth >40%

## Revenue Drivers

1. **AI server GPUs and XPUs** kept 5/4nm and 3nm at full capacity
2. **Apple iPhone 18 launch** (iPhone Duo + iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max) — inventory buildup
3. **2nm (A20 Pro)** contributed revenue for the **first time** (A20 Pro powers iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo)
4. Both wafer shipments and ASPs grew QoQ

## Apple iPhone 18 Context

- iPhone Duo = Apple's first foldable iPhone
- A20 Pro = Apple's first 2nm chip in an iPhone
- All three new iPhones (Duo, 18 Pro, 18 Pro Max) powered by A20 Pro on TSMC 2nm
