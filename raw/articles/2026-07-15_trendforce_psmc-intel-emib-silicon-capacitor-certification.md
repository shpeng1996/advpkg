# PSMC Reportedly Secures Intel EMIB Certification for Silicon Capacitors, Eyes 10K 12-inch Wafer Capacity by 2027

Published: 2026-07-15 | Source: TrendForce

## Summary

Amid soaring legacy memory prices and improving margins, Taiwanese chipmaker PSMC is making further progress in collaborations with global semiconductor leaders. According to Central News Agency and Commercial Times, PSMC has achieved key milestones in silicon interposers and silicon capacitors, with its 12-inch high-density IPD (integrated passive device) silicon capacitors securing Intel EMIB certification and entering stable production.

## Key Developments

### EMIB Silicon Capacitor Certification
- PSMC offers both 8-inch and 12-inch silicon capacitors
- 12-inch high-density IPD silicon capacitors have secured **Intel EMIB certification** and entered stable production
- 12-inch capacity targeted to exceed **10,000 wafers per month by 2027**
- 8-inch capacity expected to reach several thousand wafers

### Silicon Interposer Momentum
- PSMC is gaining momentum in silicon interposers used in TSMC's **CoWoS-S** and **CoWoS-L** advanced packaging platforms
- PSMC is benefiting from spillover demand as TSMC's CoWoS capacity expansion continues to fall short of market demand

### 3D AI Foundry Business
- PSMC's 3D AI Foundry business currently accounts for about **5% of revenue**
- Target: raise share to **20% within three years** — making it a key future growth driver
- Chairman Frank Huang: PSMC has indirectly entered NVIDIA's supply chain through power management IC and GaN products

## Micron Collaboration — Next Phase
- Following Micron's acquisition of PSMC's Tongluo fab, next phase focuses on Hsinchu site for HBM/PWF foundry services and advanced memory process development
- **PWF pilot production line**: expected completed by end of 2026; mass production targeted **Q4 2027**
- **1P DRAM process** (co-developed with Micron): equipment installation complete Q1 2027; mass production mid-2028

## Financial Results (Q2 2026)
- Q2 revenue jumped **27% QoQ** to NT$17.29 billion
- Gross margin expanded to **28%** (from 10% in Q1)
- Net profit: NT$3.29 billion; EPS: NT$0.76
- Memory accounts for more than **50% of Q2 sales**
- DRAM wafer pricing raised **45% in July** amid tightening supply; revenue impact expected from November

## Foundry Price Increases
- 8-inch and 12-inch wafer foundry prices raised **10–15% in July**
- AI servers, automotive electronics, and PMICs are strongest demand drivers
- Chairman Huang: PSMC, UMC, and Vanguard all well-positioned for gross margins **>40% in 2027**

## Industry Context
- Surging AI computing demand has driven major CSPs to lock in DRAM capacity years ahead
- DRAM shortage could extend **through 2027** (Chu/PSMC)
